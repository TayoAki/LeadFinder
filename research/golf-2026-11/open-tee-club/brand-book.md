# Open Tee Club: brand book and launch plan (draft)

**One line:** Retro rope caps for the new wave of golfers, stitched with your crew's name. Made one at a time, no minimums. Golf that is open to everyone, with a crew to play with.

**Product:** Comfort Colors Sunwashed Rope Cap (Printify 6597):
- lightweight ripstop, curved brim with a rope braid, performance sweatband, snapback;
- 6 colours: Ivory, Chambray, Peachy, Espresso, Granite, Black;
- front embroidery, made in about 3 days.

---

## 1. How we got here (the method)

1. **Start from a brand with a cult following.** Melin: its Instagram fans tag Malbon (lift 5.3), golf gear, surf and run brands, and use #golf, #dad and #family. Telfar's people tag fashion brands (Fashion Nova, Zara, Nike), #fitcheck and CultureCon.
2. **Find what they love: golf.**
3. **Find who is growing in golf:** women, and Asian, Black and Hispanic golfers (NGF 2025, below).
4. **Find what the leaders don't do.** Melin, Goorin, Ampal and Coal sell no personalised hats (0 of 1,488 products), price everything above $39, and are built for the traditional golfer.
5. **Bring the proven concepts to the growing group:**
   - the retro rope cap;
   - personalised crew caps (the best seller in every simulation);
   - plain-golf attitude slogans (the winners in both panels);
   - community (Eastside Golf's golf days, Telfar's community drops).

## 2. The audience (ICP)

**What the data says** (National Golf Foundation 2025, [participation](https://www.ngf.org/golf-participation-growing-diversifying/), [women 1-pager](https://www.ngf.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Female-1-pager-2026.pdf)):

**Golf overall**
- 29M+ on-course golfers: the 8th straight year of growth.
- Off-course players (Topgolf, simulators) are **5× more interested** in playing on a course.

**Women**
- Up **46% since 2019**, and about **60% of the net gain** in golfers.
- **35% of beginners** and **43% of off-course-only** players.
- More than half of women's net gain is **under 30**.
- About **twice as likely to lapse.** Belonging and a crew to play with is the retention job.

**Asian, Black and Hispanic golfers**
- **7.7M players**: 26% of on-course golfers and **30% of female golfers**.

**Segments** (share of the target audience; judgement informed by the data above):

| Segment | Share | What they want | Main fear |
|---|---|---|---|
| New women golfers (via friends or Topgolf) | 22% | To feel like they belong on the course, with their crew | Looking like a beginner and being judged |
| Off-course golf-curious (Topgolf, simulators) | 18% | Something fun that makes golf feel like theirs | "Golf is for country-club people" |
| Culture golfers (the Eastside Golf crowd) | 15% | Community, style, representation | Brands borrowing their culture |
| Friend-group crew organisers | 15% | A uniform for the group, easy group orders | Herding everyone's colours and payments |
| Fashion-first golf-adjacent (Telfar crowd) | 12% | A cap that works as a fit-pic piece | Novelty merch that dates |
| Women's golf group and league leaders | 6% | Team caps without 24-piece minimums | Minimum orders and long lead times |
| Gift buyers for a new golfer | 12% | A personal, encouraging gift | Getting the style wrong |

## 3. Positioning

- **Stance:** *Golf got new players; the gear still dresses the old ones. We'll never make your crew order 24 hats.*
  - Keepwatch swap test against 17 golf brands: both halves pass as **"Yours"**, meaning no competitor could say them.
  - The first half alone takes no side; the "never 24 hats" half adds one. The combined line still needs re-testing for length.
- **Tagline options:** "Your crew. Your cap." · "Open tee. Your crew." · "Golf for the group chat."
- **Voice:**
  - warm, plain, a little cheeky;
  - golf language everyone understands (bogey, OB, mulligan, birdies);
  - no borrowed slang, which both panels rejected, and no "elite" or "premium" talk.
- **Look:**
  - retro clubhouse: script and varsity lettering on Ivory, Chambray and Peachy;
  - real crews in photos: women's groups, mixed friend groups, culture-golf days and Topgolf bays.

## 6b. The brand name (same panel)

**Votes:** Open Tee Club **46**, Everybody Tee Club 30, Public Tee Club 24.

**Reactions to "Open Tee Club":**
- **Welcoming: 58**, often "if the meetups are real".
- **Generic: 37** ("sounds like a tee-time app").
- **Confused with the British Open: 3.**
- **"Club" sounds exclusive: 2.**

Only 32 felt the brand is "made for people like me", 46 were unsure and 22 said no. The name alone won't do it; **the Open Tee days and real crews in the photos have to carry the meaning.**

**Keep the name, but sharpen it:**
- the tagline does the work: *"Your crew. Your cap."*;
- lead with the community meetups in every post;
- never use Open Championship styling.

## 7. Community: Open Tee days

- Monthly meetups at a public course or Topgolf. Bring your crew or find one.
- Caps are stitched on the spot or pre-ordered.
- This copies what works: Eastside Golf's community golf days and Telfar's community-first drops.
- It also does the retention job, because women lapse twice as often and a crew keeps them playing.
- Partner with local women's golf groups and culture-golf clubs. Ambassadors come *from* the community.

## 8. Website (one page)

**Hero:** a real crew in matching Ivory caps at a Topgolf bay or on the 1st tee. Until you have real photos, use the mock-up, labelled "mock-up".

- **H1:** Caps for the crew you actually play with.
- **Sub:** Retro rope caps, stitched with your crew's name. No minimums: one cap or twelve. Made one at a time and shipped in about a week.
- **Buttons:** Design your Crew Cap ($38) · Start a crew order (everyone pays their own)

**How it works:**
1. Name your crew.
2. Pick the place and year.
3. Share the link with your crew.
4. Everyone picks a colour.
5. We stitch and ship.

**Also on the page:**
- **Open Tee days:** "Find a meetup near you, or bring your crew."
- **Classics:** the slogan caps.
- **Proof:** none at launch. Real crew photos are added as they come in, and no reviews are invented.
- **FAQ:**
  - minimums (none);
  - timing (about 3 days to make, plus shipping);
  - whether you see a preview (yes);
  - remakes if the stitching is wrong.

## 9. Wave-1 slideshows (TikTok + Instagram, one a day)

These come from the golf slideshow research (474 winning golf slideshows) and the test matrix:

1. **"5 hat ideas for your golf crew"**: crew caps; the call to action is "comment your crew name and we'll mock it up".
2. **"5 golf gifts for the new golfer in your life"**: gifts are the best-saved golf formula (10.9 saves per 1k plays). Link in bio, with a real gift cut-off date.
3. **"POV: your Topgolf crew is ready for the real course"**: the off-course to on-course bridge (people from off-course venues are 5× more interested in the course).
4. **"Every golf group has these 4"**: relatable humour is the most-shared golf formula (14.7 shares per 1k). Ends with "send this to your group chat".
5. **"Blank cap → your crew's cap"**: a before/after reveal. No golf brand runs this angle (open ground in Keepwatch).
6. **"What to wear for your first round"**: a beginner guide; tips are the biggest proven golf formula.
7. **"We stitched your crew names"**: the mock-ups from the comments on post 1.

## 10. Risks
- **Name.** "Open Tee Club" turned up no brand in Google autocomplete or the web check. Run a USPTO search in Class 25 ([tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov)). Also check that "Open" doesn't clash with The Open Championship's marks: avoid its claret jug and typography.
- **Authenticity.** Build it with the community: co-founders or ambassadors, real crews and Open Tee days. Never borrow slang or culture.
- **Unproven so far.** Per-order embroidery and the cap's cost need one Printify test order.
- **Everything here is simulated.** Post Wave 1 and take pre-orders before producing anything.
