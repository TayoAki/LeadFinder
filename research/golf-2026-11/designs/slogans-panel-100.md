## Why the top 3 won (two most common reasons among first-choice voters)

- **T13 Outside the Ropes**: Understated; suits the retro rope cap and won't date (13); Real golf language, not borrowed slang (3)
- **T2 Birdie Behavior**: Readable without explanation; safe and giftable (9); Cute/wearable on the course and off it (brunch, everyday) (3)
- **T10 Birdies & Banter**: Readable without explanation; safe and giftable (10); Real golf language, not borrowed slang (1)

## Why the bottom 3 were disliked (two most common reasons among most-disliked votes)

Bottom 3 = lowest net score (first-choice votes minus most-disliked votes). T4 Big Tee Energy (16) and T10 Birdies & Banter (13) drew more most-disliked votes but are polarising, not weak: each also has 10+ first-choice votes. T4 is disliked mainly as crude (not giftable, not for work or the club); T10 mainly as boring.

- **T6 It's Giving Birdie** (13 most-disliked): Trend slang that is already dated or will be soon (6); Shopper doesn't understand the slang (4)
- **T8 On God, On Green** (12 most-disliked): Feels like a brand borrowing a culture/voice it isn't part of (8); Religious reference feels wrong on a golf hat (2)
- **T12 The Foursome Cookout** (10 most-disliked): Feels like a brand borrowing a culture/voice it isn't part of (6); Innuendo/crude; not giftable or wearable at work/club (4)
