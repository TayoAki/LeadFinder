## Read-outs

- S04 leads on first choice (17) and buy-now (7), but most of that comes from one segment (10 of the 14 trip organisers). Group buyers held back over brand risk, not over the design.
- S09 has the most first + second votes (31) and spreads across segments: gift, style, dads, women, captains. It is the safest broad-appeal design.
- The gift-buyer and beginner "winners" carry 3/18 and 1/4 votes, so treat them as no clear winner. S01 and S06 are the weakest (S06: 0 buy-now; S01 suits only people with a real ace).
- The most common reasons for not buying are unknown-brand risk, price with shipping (about $39-43), and "I'd share it, not buy it". Real cap photos and early reviews matter more than which design you pick.
