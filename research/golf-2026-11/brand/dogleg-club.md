# Dogleg Club: brand and launch plan

**Product:** one hat, Comfort Colors Sunwashed Rope Cap (Printify blueprint 6597).

**The idea in one line:** retro rope caps with your crew's name stitched on, made one at a time, so a foursome, a golf trip or a dad and his kid can have team hats without ordering 24.

**What the buyer is:** golf-first, active, social. They play in groups, travel for golf, bring their kids and like the retro clubhouse look (Malbon). Today they can only buy a logo, not a hat that says who they play with.

**Why the rope cap fits:**
- The rope braid on a curved brim *is* the retro clubhouse look.
- The lightweight ripstop and performance sweatband work for a full round.
- It's one size, with a snapback: no "will it fit?" doubts and no exchanges.
- It's embroidered on the front, which reads as quality, and is made in about 3 days.
- It comes in 6 colours: Black, Espresso, Granite, Peachy, Chambray, Ivory.

## 2. The brand

- **Name:** Dogleg Club. A dogleg is a hole that bends; every round has one. It's retro and golf-native, with no hat or golf brand of that name in autocomplete. **Run a USPTO trademark search before buying the domain.**
- **Tagline:** *No 24-hat minimums. Your crew's name, one cap at a time.*
- **Stance:** *We will never make you order 24 hats to get your crew name on one. Stop wearing logos of tours you'll never play: your hat should say who you play with.*
  - Keepwatch swap test: **"Yours"**. A competitor couldn't say it; the closest line belongs to no tracked brand.
- **Voice:** plain, warm, clubhouse humour. It talks like the friend who organises the trip.
  - Say: "your crew", "the trip", "first round together", "made one at a time".
  - Never say: "premium", "elevate", "best-selling" (until true), or any tour or pro claim.
- **Look:**
  - Retro clubhouse: cream (Ivory) and Chambray as hero colours, varsity and script lettering, small stitched icons (flag, tee, dogleg arrow).
  - Photos show real groups in real places: the 1st tee, the cart, the 19th hole.

## 5. Ads and organic content

**Paid ad concepts.** These use the angles no golf brand runs, plus the hooks that lasted longest:
1. **Before → after (open ground):**
   - blank Ivory cap → stitched "THE BOGEY BOYS · BANDON 26" → the foursome wearing them;
   - hook: "This hat was a group chat 10 days ago."
2. **Product demo (almost open):**
   - a 10-second close-up of the rope, the stitching, the sweatband and the snapback;
   - hook: "The hat your golf trip deserves. No 24-hat minimum."
3. **Dad + kid (the 294-day hook family):**
   - matching caps, "CREW: DAD" and "CREW: CADDIE";
   - hook: "Want your kid to love golf? Give them the uniform."

Skip discount ads: 10 brands are already running them, and only 11% of sale ads last 30 days.

**Organic slideshows:** use Wave 1 from `golf-slideshow-tests.xlsx`, starting with:
- "5 hat ideas for your golf trip crew" (comment your crew name);
- "5 golf gifts they don't already own" (link in bio);
- the blank-to-stitched reveal.

**Mock-up loop:**
- "Comment your crew name and we'll mock it up." Post the best mock-ups the next day.
- Every comment is a lead, and every mock-up is a post.

**Collabs and creators** to approach (drafts only; you send them):
- **Small golf brands with a high lift among Melin's crowd:** Fairway Fits (lift 11.5), Ghost Golf, Legato Golf, Luxury Ballmarks, Sunday Golf. Offer a co-stitched crew cap. A collab hook ran 606 days.
- **Golf creators who tag gear:** many sit at 5–50k followers. Send a free Crew Cap stitched with *their* crew name, as a disclosed gift.

## 6. 14-day launch plan
1. **Days 1–2:**
   - Confirm the base cost, embroidery rules and whether per-order names work.
   - Order 3 samples (Ivory, Chambray, Black).
2. **Days 3–5:**
   - Shoot the samples on a real course with a real group.
   - Build the one-page site and the 6 Classic designs.
3. **Days 6–14:**
   - Post Wave 1 slideshows, about one a day, on TikTok and Instagram.
   - Run the mock-up loop.
   - Spend $10–20 a day on the before/after ad to a lookalike of the site's visitors and video viewers (US, golf interests).
4. **Measure:**
   - saves and shares per 1k plays;
   - mock-up comments;
   - visit → "Design your Crew Cap" clicks;
   - orders, and average hats per order (the pack test).

## 7. Risks
- **Per-order personalisation on Printify is unconfirmed.** That is the whole edge; check it first.
- **Base cost is unknown.** Margins could be thin at $34.
- **Trademarks:**
  - never stitch course logos, tour or brand names;
  - course *names* typed by customers can be trademarks, so decline the famous ones;
  - "Dogleg Club" needs a trademark search.
- **No proof at launch.** Lead with the product and the mock-ups, never invented numbers.
