# Denim Tears audience: ICP (for Open Tee Club planning)

**Sources**
- Instagram tag overlap (`ig-tags.md`): 1,050 posts tagging @denimtears by 809 accounts (196 shops), and 453 of those people's own feeds read. Totals only; at least 3 people per account.
- Web research: Tremaine Emory's brand; Black-history storytelling (cotton-wreath denim); NYC base; Tokyo store opening 2026; SCAD retrospective Sept 2026 – Jan 2027.

**What is data and what is judgement:** brand and hashtag counts are data. Segment shares, age and gender mix are judgement informed by that data; Instagram doesn't expose age or gender.

Mix (judgement): about 65% under 35; about 35–40% women; cap habit confirmed (New Era 2.3×, Supreme, Stüssy).

## What this means for Open Tee Club
1. **They're a style audience, not a golf audience.** Treat them as a later segment or a collaboration, not the launch customer.
2. **What they'd wear:** golf's own deadpan language as a wordmark (course signs, municipal-course life, club and department names, place). No jokes, no puns, no slang.
3. **The hard line: borrowing.** Nothing referencing Black history, Denim Tears' motifs or anyone else's identity. Open Tee Club's honest angle is *public golf*: municipal courses, open to anyone. That is ours to say.
4. **Test channel:** NYC-centred Instagram and TikTok fit pics, not golf content. A municipal-course fit shoot (cap, workwear, sneakers) is the native format.
