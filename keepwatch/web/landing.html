Questions, answered.

What is Keepwatch?

A tool that watches your competitors' Meta ads, shows which ones they keep paying for and which they kill, and rebuilds the winners for your brand.

Where does the ad data come from?

The public Meta Ad Library, collected through a data provider. We read a sample of each rival's ads, not their whole library, and refresh it weekly.

Is it OK to adapt a competitor's ad?

Keepwatch keeps an ad's structure and story, and never copies a rival's name, logo, claims, wording or people. You're still responsible for the ads you run, so check claims against your own product and Meta's ad rules.

Will my competitors know?

No. The Ad Library is public, and nothing we do touches their accounts.

How do credits work?

Your plan includes a monthly pool. A finished ad is 10 credits, watching a rival is 30 a month, and a buyer-panel test is 5. Failed renders are refunded automatically.

What is the Monday drop?

Every Monday at 7am your time, Keepwatch picks your rivals' longest-running ads and rebuilds four of them for your brand. If it can't (low credits, for example) it tells you why.

What is the buyer panel?

Simulated buyers built from your audience compare your ad with theirs, twice each in swapped order. It's directional: confirm with real spend.

Do I still need a designer?

Many teams run the ads as they come. Others hand them to a designer or creator as a brief. Story-led ads in the first person should be filmed with a real customer or creator.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: 14 days, 5 rivals and 200 credits, no card needed.
