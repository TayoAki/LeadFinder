# Keyword Plan

## Last Updated

## Business Context
- Offer: Shopify themes (and theme sections) we build and sell, off the Shopify Theme Store at first
- Audience: Shopify merchants choosing or replacing a theme: dropshippers, new clothing brands, one-product stores, stores on the free Horizon/Dawn themes
- Goal: sales (theme purchases)
- Positioning: not yet defined (no ./brand/positioning.md). Working angle from the data: **themes proven by published speed and conversion tests**, since every "best theme" page today is an opinion list

## The market in one paragraph
Searches for themes by niche are tiny (≤30 a month each) and generic theme searches are shrinking (−40% to −45% a year). But two independent theme brands sold off the Theme Store, **Shrine** (880 + 260 a month) and **Debutify** (880), have built real branded demand. They got there through the dropshipping creator audience, not through niche search. Search is therefore a **support channel**: win the buyer-intent comparisons that Reddit currently answers, and capture branded "alternative/review/price" demand. It won't carry the business alone.

### Demoted (failed 2+ tests)
- **Sections and templates** (sections store 90, product page template 110, landing page template 110). Volume fails, and Section Store and Replo own the results. Demoted to a product line, not a content pillar.
- **AI-built themes** ("shopify theme with ai" ~10 searches, but up sharply). Volume fails, and Shopify's own AI Store Builder takes #1. Backlog. Watch it, because it's the reason theme searches are falling.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Shopify (themes.shopify.com, shopify.com):** top 3 on nearly every generic term. Don't fight head terms.
- **Shrine and Debutify:** off-store theme brands with real branded demand, built through dropshipping YouTube. They prove the model; their review and price queries are open.
- **Fuel Themes, Halo Themes, Archetype, Out of the Sandbox:** theme sellers ranking with "best themes for X" posts, several dated 2024–2025.
- **Gaps:**
  - Reddit is #1–3 on five buyer-intent queries.
  - Nobody publishes reproducible speed or conversion test data; the best one is a 7-year-old Reddit post.
  - Horizon content is thin, and its examples question is answered by Reddit.
