## Run 1: first versions

Run 2026-11-09.

**Method:**
- **Buyers:** 6 personas built from the Weight Class audience. Each was a separate agent, blind to which ad was ours.
- **Swapped order:** each buyer saw every pair twice, as A and B, in swapped and shuffled order.
- **Ties:** a buyer whose pick flipped with the order counts as a tie. That happened 4 of 48 times.

**Read it carefully:** results are simulated and directional, and the personas come from our own target audience. Confirm with real spend.

## What the buyers said
- **Concrete beats clever.** "539 cal · 49 g protein from one photo" and "SAME CALORIES" won every buyer. Each states one clear, specific thing.
- **GLP-1 stories split the room.** The kitchen and pier-bench ads tied 3–3.
  - GLP-1 users (Linda, Teresa) chose Shotsy's original, because it names their exact medication and problem ("Day 30 on Ozempic").
  - Buyers not on a GLP-1 said "not for me" to both.
  - For a GLP-1 audience, name the medicine and the moment. Otherwise lead with food logging.
- **Recurring doubts to answer in the copy or on the image:**
  - "Is it a subscription?" Say *free* on the image, not only in the caption.
  - "Are AI photo estimates accurate?" Show that you can check or edit the numbers.
  - "Is this even an app ad?" The SAME CALORIES ad never shows the app, so add an app screen or name in the final frame.
