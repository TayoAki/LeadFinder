2026-11-09. Collected with Keepwatch, running locally.
- **Sample:** 788 public Meta Ad Library ads across 7 competitors, English-language sample, $0.16.
- **Kept** = still running after 30+ days, i.e. it survived the advertiser's own testing.
- **Killed** = stopped inside 30 days.
- **Not found:** Lose It! and MeAgain have no Meta advertiser page under any name we tried.

## What each one is doing
**Shotsy: the most direct threat. It owns GLP-1 on Meta.**
- **Theme:** 107 of 107 ads mention GLP-1, Ozempic, Zepbound or injections. No other rival comes close (Noom 15, BitePal 1).
- **Kept hooks are education plus a mistake:**
  - "I wasted 3 months of my GLP-1 journey because of this one mistake."
  - "Why does day three on a GLP-1 feel harder than day six?"
  - "Most people track their shot. Almost nobody tracks what happens after."
  - "If you're on GLP-1s, there are three non-negotiables you need to track."
- **Format:** notes-app style text over real-life UGC video, e.g. "Day 30 on Ozempic and I swear it was wearing off before my next shot. Shotsy showed me I was right."
- **Proof line:** "1M+ downloads, 4.9 stars". CTA: Download.

**Cal AI owns "count calories from a photo".**
- **Theme:** 157 ads mention photo, snap or scan.
- **Kept ad (159 days):** "AI can now count your calories, just from a photo."
- **Offer:** almost every ad is titled "Limited Time Free Trial".
- **Testing right now:** about 100 creator videos at once:
  - the founder story ("A 17-year-old just changed the fitness industry")
  - "I thought I was eating 1,800 calories… it was over 2,900"
  - "This is 100 calories"
  - "You're not overweight because you eat too much"
- **What they kill fast:** founder intros and a doctor endorsement, cut within 2–5 days.

**Noom: the long game.**
- **Its two oldest ads:**
  - "There is no way I'm going back to my high weight after this." (599 days, still live)
  - "Take our FREE 5-minute quiz…" (510 days)
- **Format:** most of its kept ads are static images.
- **Newest tests are GLP-1:** "Noom combines GLP-1 medications with behavior change…". Noom is moving into Weight Class's space.

**MyFitnessPal: few ads, but they last.**
- **Format:** calorie comparisons ("This is 150 calories" ×6) and "me before tracking vs after".
- **Message:** "#1 Nutrition Tracking App" and "Track your food with ease".
- **They also run partner ads** (Campbell's protein soup).

**BitePal and YAZIO:**
- **BitePal:** runs a sarcastic raccoon character ("your raccoon keeps you accountable, sarcastically") with a quiz funnel, mostly in Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.
- **YAZIO:** testing without keepers.

## What this means for Weight Class
1. **Nobody combines the two winning messages.** Shotsy owns GLP-1 but isn't a food logger. Cal AI owns the photo logger but never mentions GLP-1. Weight Class is both, and no rival's ads say so: "Snap your plate. Built for GLP-1 appetites."
2. **"Free" is not crowded the way you'd think.** Cal AI says "free trial", which means a paywall. Nobody says "free, no trial". Weight Class's free barcode scanning and free GLP-1 mode is a real differentiator; lead with it.
3. **Borrow Shotsy's format, not its words.** Notes-app text over UGC, an education hook, and "the mistake" framing are what Shotsy keeps paying for. Point them at food instead of shots:
   - "I wasn't eating enough protein on Zepbound and didn't know it."
   - "No food sounds good on shot day. Here's what to eat."

   These come from the protein-on-a-small-appetite gap in feature-gap-research.md.
4. **Borrow Cal AI's demo, then go further.** "Count calories from a photo" is proven at 159 days. Weight Class's version: the same snap, plus the protein and fiber you need on GLP-1.
5. **Open ground nobody runs:**
   - **Taste and food-first creative:** no rival shows appetising food on a small appetite.
   - **Urgency:** nobody uses it.
   - **Social proof:** burning out; the only social-proof ad stopped.

   These are hypotheses to test, not proof.
6. **Watch Noom.** It's adding GLP-1 ads now. Re-run Keepwatch weekly to see whether those survive past 30 days.

## Caveats
- **Samples, not totals:** each rival's numbers come from a sample of their public ads, never their whole library.
- **Lasting isn't selling:** run length shows an ad lasted, not that it made money.
- **BitePal's transcripts are unreliable:** many are song lyrics or other languages, so its "kept" ads are read from the visuals and titles.
- **Automatic angle tags are rough on app ads,** e.g. Shotsy is tagged "expert endorsement" because its copy says "recommend". The readings above come from the hooks, copy and transcripts themselves.
