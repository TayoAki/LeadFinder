# Weight Class: ad descriptions and hashtags

Written 2026-11-13 for [Weight Class on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/weight-class/id6816738487).

**Sources:**
- Keepwatch competitor ads (`competitor-ads.md`): 788 ads across 7 rivals.
- The simulated ad test (`ad-test-results.md`).
- Search and Reddit gaps (`feature-gap-research.md`).

Every claim below matches the current App Store listing:
- Free to use, with 3 AI scans a day.
- Barcode, USDA search and GLP-1 mode are free.
- Premium is $7.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a one-month trial for new subscribers.

## What the data says to write

1. **Nobody says "GLP-1" and "snap your plate" in the same ad.**
   - Shotsy runs GLP-1 in all 107 of its ads but isn't a food logger.
   - Cal AI's photo ad has run 159 days, but its ads never mention GLP-1.
   - Weight Class is the only app that does both. Say both.
2. **Say "free" in the copy.** Cal AI's "free trial" means a paywall. "Is it a subscription?" was the top objection in the ad test; saying *free* turned the ties into wins.
3. **Concrete beats clever.** Ads that name one specific number ("539 cal · 49 g protein from one photo") won every buyer in the test.
4. **Name the moment for GLP-1 users.** Shot day, day 3, appetite coming back. For everyone else, lead with food logging; non-GLP-1 buyers said "not for me" to injection ads.
5. **Answer "is the AI accurate?"** Say that you can check and change any number, and that numbers come from the USDA database.
6. **Searches are moving from brand names to the class.** "glp-1" is up 1.47×, "ozempic" down to 0.60×. So the copy says **GLP-1** and leaves drug brand names to hashtags. (Brand names did win GLP-1 users in the simulated test, so keep a "Zepbound" or "Ozempic" variant for a real A/B test.)

## Description options

### A. GLP-1 plus the photo log (the open ground; lead with these)
1. On a GLP-1, every bite has to count. Snap your plate and Weight Class shows the protein, fiber and calories, then tracks your doses and side effects in the same free app.
2. Most people only log the shot. Weight Class logs what happens after: snap a meal, see your protein, and spot the days fiber and water ran low. Free on iPhone.
3. Small appetite, big protein goal? Take a photo of what you managed to eat and see exactly how far you are from today's protein target. GLP-1 mode is free.
4. Shot day, dose day, the days nothing sounds good. Log your meals in one photo and your doses in one tap, free. (Weight Class never gives dosing advice.)

### B. The photo log for everyone (proven by Cal AI; we add "free" and "you can check it")
5. Snap your meal. Get the calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber in seconds, with numbers from the USDA food database. Free to use on iPhone.
6. One photo: 539 cal, 49 g protein. Wrong portion? Change it in a tap, and Weight Class remembers it next time.
7. No photo? Describe it in a few words. Packaged food? Scan the barcode, free. Logging a meal has never been this fast.

### C. Free, no paywall (the gap MyFitnessPal left open)
8. Barcode scanning shouldn't cost money. In Weight Class it's free, and so are food search and GLP-1 mode, plus 3 AI photo scans a day.
9. Tired of paying to scan a barcode? Weight Class is free to use. Upgrade only if you want unlimited AI scans.

### D. Calm and kind (a trust line, not a headline)
10. Track calories, protein, fiber and water, and nothing you didn't ask for. Calm mode hides the numbers on the days counting feels like too much.

### E. Creator / notes-app style (the format Shotsy keeps paying for; post from the creator's account)
11. Week 6 on a GLP-1. I realised I was hitting 40 g of protein a day, not 100. Now I snap every plate and it tells me exactly what's missing. #ad
12. Things I wish I'd tracked from day one on my GLP-1: protein, fiber, water. I do all three with one photo now, and it's free. #ad

**Rules for every option:**
- Don't add before-and-after photos, weight-loss promises, or copy that shames bodies. Meta restricts weight-loss ads, and they must be targeted 18+.
- Keep the "never gives dosing advice" line anywhere doses are mentioned.

## Hashtags

**Use 3–5 per post.** Rivals keep their winning ads light on tags.

| Use | Hashtags | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GLP-1 audience | `#glp1` `#glp1journey` `#glp1community` | The class name searches are moving to. |
| Medicine names (organic and creator posts only) | `#zepbound` `#mounjaro` `#wegovy` `#ozempic` | Shotsy's GLP-1 users responded to the named medicine. Keep these out of paid ad copy. |
| Food logging | `#caloriecounter` `#calorietracking` `#macros` `#proteingoals` | Cal AI and MyFitnessPal territory; "protein on GLP-1" searches are up 5×. |
| Our angle | `#highprotein` `#fiber` `#foodlog` `#iphoneapp` | Answers the small-appetite protein and fiber gaps. |
| Creator posts | `#ad` | Required on paid creator posts. |

**Ready-made sets:**
- **GLP-1 creator video:** `#ad #glp1 #glp1journey #proteingoals #zepbound`
- **Paid photo-log ad:** `#caloriecounter #macros #highprotein`
- **Free / no-paywall post:** `#caloriecounter #foodlog #iphoneapp`
- **GLP-1 organic post:** `#glp1 #glp1community #highprotein #fiber`
