# Lead Magnet System — ListingProof (working name)

Read first: [differentiators.md](differentiators.md), [uvp.md](uvp.md), [angles.md](angles.md), [voice.md](voice.md), [offer.md](offer.md). The magnet is for visitors who **don't** start a free kit (no listing right now, or not ready). The free kit itself is the product-led path; this is the catch net. REC proves the pattern in this category (exit-intent popup to Klaviyo, teardown §2.2).

## Problem architecture
- **Narrow problem:** "I don't know what a listing ad that actually works looks like *in my market*." Small, tactical, solved in one sitting by seeing the 5 listing ads agents nearby have kept running longest, with why each one lasts.
- **Wide problem it exposes:** turning *every* new listing into ads and videos built on those patterns — with your real photos, nothing invented, labelled where needed, without a videographer or a subscription — each time, all year. That is exactly the Listing Kit / Team plan.
- **Bridge sentence:** *"Great — you now know what lasts in your market. But who's going to rebuild that for your next listing, with your photos, by Friday, without inventing a room?"*

Failure test: solving the narrow problem (knowing the patterns) leaves the build undone → something to sell ✓. Seeing five concrete local winners naturally raises "can I make mine like that?" → pull ✓.

## Format decision
Quadrant: **top-left (high engagement, easy to consume)** because the offer is simple and affordable ($49 per listing, $199 team), and we want volume. A top-right webinar would be wrong for a $49 product.
Format: **"Your Local Listing Ad Report"** — a personalized one-page web report for the visitor's metro (a toolkit, not a PDF ebook), plus a 6-minute screen-recorded walkthrough of how to read it.

Contents (generated by Keepwatch from the public Meta Ad Library; metro discovery is BUILD):
1. The 5 listing ads in your metro that agents have kept running longest (run days, start date, format), each linked to its public Ad Library page — we **link**, we don't re-host their creative or name-shame anyone.
2. For each: the structure in one line (hook type, first shot, caption style, CTA) and why it likely lasted.
3. The pattern across all five ("4 of 5 open on the exterior; 3 of 5 put the price in the first line").
4. A fill-in hook template for your next listing, built from the strongest pattern.
5. A 6-item fair-housing and disclosure check for whatever you post (brokerage name visible; Housing special ad category; describe the property, not the buyer; label virtual staging/enhancement; keep the original photo; no "perfect for families"-type phrases). Short, practical, and sourced to Meta's special-ad-category docs and the state/MLS pages linked in the SaaS deep-dive.
6. Footer CTA: "Want this built for your next listing? Your first kit is free."

Thin-metro rule: if fewer than 3 kept listing ads exist in the metro, the report says so plainly and uses the nearest larger metro, named. We don't pad.

## Landing page (full copy)
**Headline:** "See the 5 listing ads that lasted longest in [your city] — and the pattern behind them"
*(fuses the two wants: proof of what works locally + a template for my next listing)*

**Image direction:** a phone mockup showing a one-page report titled "Listing ads that lasted: Austin, TX" (example metro) with five ad thumbnails blurred except the run-day badges ("412 days", "214 days"…), plus a sticky note style tag "+ your hook template". Thumbnails in the mockup must be our own sample ads, not real agents' ads.

**Transformation copy (3 lines):**
- Stop guessing what to post — see what agents near you kept paying to run.
- Write your next listing hook in five minutes from the strongest local pattern.
- Post with confidence: a six-point fair-housing and disclosure check on one page.

**Form fields:** Email · City or ZIP. (Two fields, not one: the city is used to build the report. First name is not collected because nothing uses it.)

**Trust builders:** none yet — page designed without them. No counts, no stars, no logos. One honest line instead: "Built from the public Meta Ad Library. Updated [date]. We link to every ad we mention."

**CTA button text:** "Send my local report"

Placement: `/local-ads` page for ads and the article; exit-intent and 20-seconds-on-page popup on the home page (not at 3 seconds, which interrupts the free-kit path). One CTA, no nav on `/local-ads`.

Fair-housing note: the report covers ads *for* listings; the landing page and report never describe or target buyers by protected class.

**Q3. How do you make listing videos and ads today?** (pick the closest)
- Brokerage tools or Canva templates
- Myself in CapCut or on my phone

**Q4 (open). "If a tool made your next listing's video and ads, what would it have to get right for you to pay for it?"**

Tool recommendation: **Typeform** (conditional logic so "I mostly work with buyers" skips Q3, and answers pass to the email tool as fields). Google Forms is free but can't pass answers to segments cleanly; RightMessage is overkill pre-launch. If the email tool has native forms with custom fields (e.g., Kit), use that instead and skip Typeform.

## What the survey will let you decide
| Question | Decision it unlocks |
|---|---|
| Q1 listings/yr | Which rung to pitch first: buyers' agents → nurture only (they self-excluded); 2–15 → Listing Kit; 16+ / team → Team plan. Also tests the "listing-heavy" audience assumption in uvp.md |
| Q2 challenge | Which email track (shadow newsletter) and which ad angle wins on intent: "different" → Angle 1; "AI changes house" → Angle 2; "time"/"videographer" → Angle 3 math; "clicks not conversations" → the DFY tier and a future results feature |
| Q3 current method | The comparison we must win: free templates (beat on output), DIY (beat on time), AI app (beat on accuracy + billing), videographer (beat on price per listing) |
| Q4 open text | Raw language for ads and the next product (e.g., results tracking, avatar demand). Read every answer weekly until 200 responses |

## Expected numbers and what they mean (UNSOURCED bands — replace with our own after 30 days)
- `/local-ads` visit → email: 25–40% expected for a personalized, two-field page (UNSOURCED). Below 15% = headline/offer problem (test the headline first). Above 15% but few report opens = delivery/deliverability problem.
- Popup view → email: 2–5% (UNSOURCED). Below 1% = timing or offer mismatch.
- Survey completion on the thank-you page: 40–60% (UNSOURCED). Below 25% = too long; cut Q3.
- Report → free kit started within 14 days: 5–10% (UNSOURCED). Below 3% with good opens = the bridge (email 2) isn't landing, not the magnet.

## Assumptions & Unknowns
- Metro discovery of agent advertisers is BUILD. Until it exists, reports are produced semi-manually for the top 25 US metros and cached; other cities get "nearest metro" plus a note.
- Data cost per metro report (Ad Library pulls via the scraping provider) is unknown; cache per metro, refresh monthly.
- Meta's terms restrict automated collection of Ad Library data (Keepwatch README "Data use"). Check fit before scaling. We link to ads rather than re-host them.
