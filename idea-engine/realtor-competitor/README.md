# ListingProof — go-to-market summary (2026-11-10)

A direct competitor to VideoTour.ai and RealEstateContent.ai, built on the Keepwatch engine. **ListingProof is a working name and a placeholder** — domain and trademark availability not checked.

## Positioning
- **Who:** US listing agents (about one or more listings a month) and small teams (3–15 agents). Not buyers' agents, not avatar/lifestyle-scene seekers, not brokerages wanting white-label.
- **UVP (short):** Listing videos and Meta ads built from your real photos, modeled on the listing ads agents in your market have kept running. Nothing invented. Pay per listing. → [uvp.md](uvp.md)
- **Enemy:** the template-and-guesswork, annual-lock-in way of marketing listings.
- **Angle:** Local Proof (mechanism) leads; Nothing Invented (truth) is the first challenger. Market sophistication Stage 3 → 4. → [angles.md](angles.md)
- **Voice:** plain, exact, calm, on the agent's side; every number sourced or tagged. → [voice.md](voice.md)

## Differentiator
**POSITIONAL (~12-month shelf life):** each kit's videos and ads are modeled on listing ads that agents in the buyer's metro have kept running 30+ days (public Meta Ad Library, ranked by run length), rebuilt with the agent's own photos. Neither competitor sells ads or models local winners. Nothing is structural; supporting points (truthful motion with originals shown, pay-per-listing, honest billing) are proof, not the differentiator. Deficits: no reviews or user counts, no avatars, no social autopilot, no portal-link import. → [differentiators.md](differentiators.md)
