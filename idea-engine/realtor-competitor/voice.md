## Voice Summary
ListingProof sounds like a sharp, honest colleague who has looked at the data and will show you the receipts. Short sentences. Real numbers with a source or none at all. Calm about AI, strict about accuracy, plain about money.

## Core Personality Traits
- **Exact:** Says what it does and what it doesn't. Numbers carry a source or a date. "Kept running 412 days" beats "proven".
- **On the agent's side:** Talks about the agent's liability, their seller, their slow months. Never blames the agent for past bad tools.
- **Calm, not hyped:** No exclamation marks in body copy. No urgency we can't back with a real date.
- **Shows the work:** Points to the original photo, the source ad's run length, the billing date. Proof over adjectives.

## Vocabulary

**Words/phrases to USE:**
- "Your real photos" / "your listing photos" — anchors truthfulness
- "Kept running" / "lasted" — our plain term for ads live 30+ days; always explain once: "still running after 30+ days, which usually means the agent kept paying for it"
- "Modeled on" — never "copied from"; we copy structure, not content
- "Per listing" / "$49 a listing"
- "Here's the original" — next to any clip
- "Nothing invented"
- "Simulated" — whenever the buyer panel is mentioned
- "Your market" / "agents near you" — never name the agents whose ads we modeled in marketing copy

**Words/phrases to AVOID:**
- "Autopilot", "set it and forget it" — competitor framing, and it's not what we do
- "Stunning", "cinematic", "magic", "wow" — hype; also category clichés
- "Guaranteed results", "more showings", "sell faster", "2x faster" — we cannot prove these
- "100% MLS compliant", "fully compliant" — no tool can promise this; say "built to help you meet…" and list the checks
- "AI-powered", "cutting-edge", "leverage", "unlock", "supercharge", "game-changer", "seamless"
- Any phrase lifted from VideoTour or REC copy
- Fair-housing risk words in any example listing copy: "perfect for families", "safe neighborhood", "exclusive community", "walking distance to church", "great for young professionals", "bachelor pad"

**Jargon level:** Light, and only the agent's own jargon (MLS, listing appointment, just listed, special ad category). Explain "kept running" and "special ad category" once per piece.

**Profanity:** Never.

## Rhythm & Structure

**Sentences:** Mostly 5–15 words. One longer sentence per paragraph at most. Fragments allowed for emphasis.

**Paragraphs:** 1–3 sentences. Lots of white space.

**Openings:** Lead with a concrete fact or the agent's situation ("New listing Friday. Photographer's files land Thursday night."). Rarely a question; never "Are you looking for…".

**Formatting:** Headers that say what's below. Short bullet lists. Tables for prices and comparisons. Bold sparingly. No emojis in body copy; at most one in an ad's first line, and only if it carries meaning (→, ✓).

## POV & Address

**First person:** "We" for the company. "I" only in founder notes signed by Tayo.
**Reader address:** "You", direct.
**Relationship stance:** Guide with receipts. A peer who did the research so the agent doesn't have to.

## Example Phrases

**On-brand (sounds like us):**
- "Your seller's house, not an AI's guess. Here's the original photo next to every clip."
- "This hook comes from a listing ad an agent in your area has kept running for 214 days. We kept the structure and used your photos."
- "$49 a listing. If you don't list in March, you don't pay in March."
- "We'll email you 3 days before any charge. Cancel in one click from the billing page."
- "The buyer panel is simulated — AI personas, not real buyers. Treat it as a tiebreaker, not a forecast."

**Off-brand (doesn't sound like us):**
- "Turn boring listings into STUNNING cinematic masterpieces in seconds! 🚀🔥" — hype, emoji wall, cliché
- "Join 10,000+ agents selling homes 2x faster!" — invented count, unprovable claim
- "Our AI-powered platform leverages cutting-edge technology to supercharge your listing marketing." — corporate AI speak
- "Only 3 spots left at this price!" — fake urgency
- "Perfect for families looking for a safe neighborhood." — fair-housing risk

## Do's and Don'ts

**DO:**
- Put a source, a date or a "simulated"/"UNSOURCED" tag on every number
- Show the original photo or the source ad's run length wherever we make a claim about output
- State price, billing period and cancel method in the same sentence block
- Use placeholders like [CUSTOMER QUOTE, to collect] until real, named, consented quotes exist
- Describe properties by features, never by who should live there

**DON'T:**
- Promise leads, showings, faster sales or compliance outcomes
- Name competitors in ads (comparison articles may, factually, with dates)
- Copy competitor wording, even to mock it
- Use first-name-only testimonials or star ratings we don't have
- Write urgency without a real, enforced deadline
