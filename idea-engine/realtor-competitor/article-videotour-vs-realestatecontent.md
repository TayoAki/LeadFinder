VideoTour.ai and RealEstateContent.ai get compared because agents see both in their Facebook feed, usually in the same week. They don't do the same job.

**VideoTour.ai turns listing photos into video.** **RealEstateContent.ai runs your social media posting.** If you need a tour for Friday's listing, one of them is the wrong tool. If you need to post four times a week whether or not you have a listing, it's the other way round.

The bigger differences are in how they bill you. One shows an annual price by default and doesn't refund. The other shows the monthly price and gives you 14 days to get your money back. That matters more than any feature list, because billing is what agents complain about most in this category.

## Quick verdict

**Choose VideoTour.ai if** you list regularly, want a video from your photos in minutes, and you're fine committing to a plan once the free video convinces you. Pick monthly billing on purpose. Its pricing page opens on annual.

**Choose RealEstateContent.ai if** your problem is posting consistently, not making listing videos. You want one flat plan that does posts, captions, market updates and some video, and you're comfortable paying $119 a month without a free trial (with a 14-day refund window as the safety net).

**Choose neither yet if** you take a handful of listings a year and your brokerage already gives you a video tool. Try that first. It's free.

## VideoTour.ai: what it is and what it costs

### What it does

You upload listing photos (up to 60) or paste a listing link from Zillow and several other portals. A few minutes later you get a video tour. There's a feature menu on top: Reels, an AI avatar that "shows up" for you, voiceover, 3D captions, lifestyle scenes, transitions, virtual staging, drone-style shots and a logo intro. Each of those has its own landing page, and most are paid for with AI credits rather than the plan itself.

The free offer is real and generous by category standards. After you confirm your email you get one video and 50 AI credits, once, with no card. The landing page repeats "No credit card required" four times, and they mean it.

One catch on the free video, from their own help center: you can't add or replace photos after creating the free tour, and deleting it doesn't give you another one. So pick your best photos before you hit create.

### What it costs

This is where you need to read slowly.

The [pricing page](https://videotour.ai/pricing) loads with the **Annual** toggle already selected. That's not an opinion; the toggle's HTML has `aria-pressed="true"` on Annual when the page loads. So the first prices you see are $29, $59 and $99 "per month".

Those are annual plans. VideoTour's own [help center](https://videotour.ai/help-center/articles/manage-your-plan) says it plainly: "Independent Annual displays the equivalent of $29 per month, but charges $348 for the year." The monthly prices are **$39, $79 and $159**.

| Plan | Videos a month | Users | Monthly billing | Annual billing (charged upfront) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Independent | 10 | 1 | $39/month | $348/year |
| Agency | 40 | 2 | $79/month | $708/year |
| Enterprise | 200 | 5 | $159/month | $1,188/year |

On top of that, AI credit packs run $5, $9, $29 and $99 (100 to 3,500 credits). Credits never expire. Unused videos in your monthly allowance don't roll over.

### Refunds and cancelling

From the [terms](https://videotour.ai/terms-of-service), section 10: "Payments for subscriptions and credit packs are not refundable once made, including for a completed video you are not satisfied with. We refund billing errors, such as a duplicate charge, and proven technical failures attributable to VideoTour AI LLC."

To be fair to them: failed AI generations return their credits automatically, and duplicate charges get refunded. What you can't get back is money for a video you just don't like, or the rest of an annual plan you stop using. Subscriptions renew automatically, and cancelling stops the next renewal. "The remaining months are not refunded."

Our take: the product's free video does the selling, and it's a good free offer. The annual-first display is the part we'd change. It's legal and it's disclosed in the help center, but when your billing complaint is the most common one in the category, it's a choice that will cost them trust.

### Accuracy

The landing page says "AI moves the camera. AI never touches the room" and "100% MLS Compliant". Their [help center](https://videotour.ai/help-center/articles/fix-unwanted-ai-changes) is more careful, and more useful. It explains that when an AI camera move reveals part of a room the photo never showed, "the tool has to estimate what belongs there. That can produce an extra door, furniture, a changed window or an incorrect room shape. Treat the result as a draft."

That's honest, and it's true of every AI video tool that moves a camera through a still photo. It also means the checking is on you. Their [MLS help article](https://videotour.ai/help-center/articles/commercial-use-and-mls) adds: "A watermark-free export is not automatically an MLS-compliant video."

If you're in California, this isn't optional. [AB 723](https://therealdeal.com/san-francisco/2026/01/26/ab-723-takes-effect-cracking-down-on-fake-listing-photos), in effect since January 2026, requires disclosure of digitally altered listing images and access to the original. Agents on Reddit also describe MLS fines for altered media ("automatic $2500 fine… No warning, no appeal", in [this thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/RealEstateTechnology/comments/1p3b1bm/)). Compare every AI clip to the original photo before it goes anywhere near the MLS.

### Who VideoTour.ai fits

Agents who list often enough to use 10 videos a month, who like trying effects, and who are happy to check each video against the photos. Teams who want avatars without filming. If you list three homes a year, the Independent plan is 120 videos a year for three listings.

## RealEstateContent.ai: what it is and what it costs

### What it does

RealEstateContent.ai is a social media tool. You describe your ideal client, it suggests content ideas, writes posts, makes images and some image-to-video, and puts them on a calendar across Facebook, Instagram, X and LinkedIn. The plan also lists market reports, blog posts, listing-to-social posts, Canva integration and an "AI Twin". Teams get a quote.

Listing video is one feature among many, not the product. If the question in your head is "how do I make a video tour of 123 Main Street", this isn't built around that.

### What it costs

One plan. The [pricing page](https://www.realestatecontent.ai/pricing-table/) headline says it directly: "One plan, everything included, and you pick how you pay."

| Billing | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $119/month | "Billed month to month. Cancel any time, no contract." |
| Annual | $999/year | Shown against $1,428 ("nearly four months free"); adds onboarding and weekly live training |

The monthly price is shown first. Good.

One thing we noticed and couldn't resolve: two of its long-running Meta ads say "For just $99" (one has been live for 481 days in the [Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1198179202115071)), while the pricing page they link to shows $119. The ad links carry a promo code. We didn't go through checkout, so we can't tell you whether the code brings it to $99. Check the amount on the checkout screen before you pay.

### Refunds and cancelling

There's no free trial. Instead there's a 14-day money-back guarantee, and the co-founder signs a note on the pricing page promising to "personally issue a full refund". The [terms](https://www.realestatecontent.ai/terms/) put it more formally: fees for the first subscription term "may be refundable under our 14-day money-back guarantee, if you request a refund within fourteen (14) days after the start of your first" term. After that, cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period. The FAQ says you can cancel "from your account in a couple of clicks, without a phone call."

The refund covers your first term only. If you take the annual plan and stop using it in month four, the remaining months aren't covered by that guarantee.

### Who RealEstateContent.ai fits

Agents whose real problem is consistency: they know they should post, they don't, and they'd pay $119 a month to have it handled. It's a weaker fit if you mostly need listing-specific video, or if you don't want a monthly bill in the months you're not actively marketing.

## Head-to-head: which one for which agent

**"I have a listing going live Friday and need a video."** VideoTour.ai. Use the free video first. If it works, buy one month of Independent at $39, not the $348 annual, until you know you'll use it.

**"I need to post four times a week and I never do."** RealEstateContent.ai, monthly. Use the 14 days properly: set up a full month of posts in week one, so you know whether you'll keep using it before the refund window closes.

**"I list two or three homes a year."** Probably neither as a subscription. Your brokerage may already include listing video. KW Command, Compass and Lofty's Social Agent all offer some version of it, and many brokerages give agents Canva. Use what you already pay for.

**"I run a team of eight."** VideoTour's Agency plan covers 2 users and 40 videos a month at $79. RealEstateContent.ai quotes teams separately. Ask both for the per-agent cost in writing, and ask what happens to the content if an agent leaves.

**"I'm in California or a strict MLS."** Either can work, but you are responsible for disclosure. Keep the original photos, label anything virtually staged or enhanced, and don't upload a video to the MLS until you've watched it next to the photos.

## What we saw in their ads

We looked at 210 of their Meta ads in the public Ad Library (130 from VideoTour.ai, 80 from RealEstateContent.ai) on November 10, 2026. Two patterns are worth knowing as a buyer.

VideoTour.ai's longest-running ads are its plainest: "Turn up to 20 photos into a cinematic listing video in just 2 minutes" and "Try it for FREE". [One](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=4240123692877099) has run 479 days. It also runs short sale ads (Black Friday "40% off for life" in 2025) that last a few days each. If you're about to buy in late November, wait a week and see.

RealEstateContent.ai runs almost one message: "Join thousands of agents using AI to put social media on autopilot", across 73 ads. Nearly every ad sends you straight to the pricing page. Its two longest video ads are agent testimonials about leads from social media. We can't verify those claims, and neither can you, so judge the product on the 14 days, not the ad.

Neither company shows independent reviews. We found no Trustpilot or G2 profile with real volume for either; a search result for RealEstateContent.ai's G2 page showed zero reviews. Both use first-name testimonials on their own sites. That doesn't mean the products are bad. It means the proof is thin, so the free video and the refund window are your real evaluation tools.

## What neither one does

Neither tells you whether the video or post worked. No views, leads or showings per listing video in the product, as far as their public pages show. RealEstateContent.ai's homepage says "90% See results in 30 days" without saying what a result is. VideoTour.ai's page says "Videos proven to sell homes 2x quicker" without a source.

Neither builds Meta **ads** for your listing. You get a video or a post. Turning it into an ad in the Housing special ad category, with your brokerage name where your state requires it, is still your job.

And neither is priced per listing. If you list in spring and not in winter, you pay the same in January as in April.

## FAQ

### Is VideoTour.ai free?
You get one free video and 50 AI credits, once, after confirming your email. No card. After that, plans start at $39 a month billed monthly, or $348 a year billed upfront (shown as "$29/month").

### How much is VideoTour.ai per month?
$39, $79 or $159 a month on monthly billing, for 10, 40 or 200 videos. The $29, $59 and $99 figures are annual plans divided by 12 and charged upfront.

### Does VideoTour.ai give refunds?
Not for completed videos you're unhappy with or for unused time. Its terms say payments "are not refundable once made", except billing errors and proven technical failures. Failed AI generations return credits automatically.

### How much does RealEstateContent.ai cost?
$119 a month, or $999 a year. There is one plan. Team pricing is by quote.

### Does RealEstateContent.ai have a free trial?
No. It has a 14-day money-back guarantee on your first term instead.

### Can I use these videos on the MLS?
Check your MLS rules first. VideoTour's own help center says a watermark-free export "is not automatically an MLS-compliant video". Remove branding and spoken contact details if your MLS requires an unbranded video, and disclose any AI enhancement.

## Bottom line

They're different tools. VideoTour.ai makes listing videos and has the better free offer; read the billing toggle before you pay. RealEstateContent.ai runs your social posting and has the fairer refund; make sure posting is really your problem before you start the 14 days.
