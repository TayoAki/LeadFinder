### 6. FAQ
**Is the free kit really free?** Yes. One listing, no card. We need your email to send it. Unlimited re-renders and photo swaps for 14 days.

**What does "kept running" mean?** An ad that's still live in Meta's public Ad Library after 30 days or more. Agents usually switch off ads that don't work, so lasting is a good sign it paid its way. It's not proof of sales, and we don't claim it is.

**Do you copy other agents' ads?** We copy structure only: the opening shot, the order, the caption style. Never their photos, footage, names or wording. Your kit uses your photos and your brand.

**Will AI change the house?** Not in the default kit. The camera moves over your real photo; no new pixels are generated. If you choose an optional AI clip, it's labelled "virtually enhanced", shown next to the original, and covered by the accuracy promise.

**Is it MLS compliant?** No tool can promise that, because MLS rules differ. We give you an unbranded cut with no logos, contact details or voiceover, the original photos alongside, and a label on anything enhanced. Check your MLS's rules before uploading.

**What about California's AB 723?** AB 723 requires a disclosure on digitally altered listing images and access to the unaltered original. If anything in your kit goes beyond camera motion over your photo, we label it and link the original. Confirm how your broker and MLS apply the law; we're not lawyers.

**Can I run the ads myself?** Yes. Each ad comes with a headline, primary text and a call to action, plus a one-page guide to launching it in Ads Manager under the Housing special ad category. Or choose done-for-you and we set it up in your account.

**Who sees the targeting?** Housing ads on Meta can't target by age, gender or ZIP code. That's why the creative matters. Our wording check flags phrases that describe who should live in a home ("perfect for families") so the ad describes the home instead.

**How does billing work?** Listing Kits and done-for-you are one-time charges. Team is monthly, with an email three days before every charge and one-click cancel. No annual plan right now.

**Can I get a refund?** Your first payment, within 30 days, no questions. And any kit that shows something not in your photos, any time.

**Do you do avatars, virtual staging or lifestyle scenes?** No. We don't put things in a house that aren't there. If you need staging, use a staging service and disclose it.

**Do you track views and leads?** Not yet. We'll say so on this page when we do.

**Do I own the videos?** Yes, the kit is yours to use for marketing this listing. You need the right to use the listing photos you upload (usually from your photographer's licence).

**Is this for agents outside the US?** Not yet. Our local data and ad rules are US only.
