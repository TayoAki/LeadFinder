## Step 1 — Value statement
> We help US listing agents and small teams turn each new listing's real photos into vertical videos and Meta ads modeled on the listing ads agents in their own market have kept running, so they can market every listing like a top local agent — without a videographer, without AI inventing anything in the house, and without paying in months they don't list.

Self-exclusion test — who correctly walks away:
- Buyer's agents and agents with no listings (nothing to upload).
- Agents who want AI lifestyle scenes, virtual furniture or an avatar that talks for them (we refuse to invent).
- Agents whose problem is daily posting cadence, not listings (REC-style autopilot fits them better).
- Brokerages shopping for an enterprise white-label licence.
- Agents outside the US (special-ad-category and fair-housing rules differ; we model US metros only).

## Step 4 — Movement / enemy statement
> Listing-video apps sell every agent the same templates, let AI guess what's in rooms it never saw, and bill you a year up front. That means your listing looks like everyone else's, one invented doorway can cost you an MLS fine, and you keep paying in months you have nothing to list. Until now.

Enemy = the template-and-guesswork, annual-lock-in way of marketing listings (an industry default), not a person or a named company.

## Step 5 — Assembled UVP
**[MOVEMENT]** Listing-video apps sell every agent the same templates, let AI guess what's in rooms it never saw, and bill a year up front — so your listing looks like everyone else's, one invented doorway can cost you a fine, and you pay in months you don't list.
**[ASPIRATION + DEEP DIVE]** There's a simpler way to make a listing look like the good ones in your town: start from what's already working there.
**[VALUE, TWISTED]** ListingProof takes your real listing photos and builds the tour reel, the hook reel and a set of Meta ads modeled on the listing ads agents in your own market have kept paying to run for 30 days or more — made for listing agents and small teams who'd rather be accurate than flashy.
**[PROOF]** *(omitted — no customers yet. Replace with a real, named, consented result once one exists: [CUSTOMER QUOTE, to collect].)*
**[OBJECTIONS]** Every clip sits next to the photo it came from, and if anything shows up that isn't in your photos we redo it or refund it. You pay $49 when you have a listing — no subscription, no credits, no annual plan — your first kit is free with no card, it takes about five minutes of your time, and your first payment is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Objection trace: fake/fine → "redo it or refund it" + side by side · generic → "modeled on… your own market" · annual trap → "no subscription… no annual plan" + money-back · don't list monthly → "pay $49 when you have a listing" · video doesn't sell → "look like the good ones in your town" (we do not claim it sells homes) · no time → "about five minutes of your time".

Substitution test: put "VideoTour.ai" in the [VALUE] clause → false (no local-winner modeling, no ads, no per-listing price). Put "RealEstateContent.ai" → false (no listing kit, $119/mo plan). **Passes.**

## Assumptions & Unknowns
- Objections and aspirations are hypotheses from public reviews/Reddit, not customer interviews.
- "About five minutes of your time" is a design target for the upload flow (BUILD), **UNSOURCED** until timed on real users.
- The `OURS` element is POSITIONAL with ~12-month shelf life; re-check competitors in May 2027.
- Metro coverage: if a metro has fewer than 3 kept listing ads, the kit says it modeled on the nearest larger market or the national kept set. The UVP's "your own market" must not be shown to users whose kit didn't use local data.
- "Listing-heavy" threshold (≈1 listing/month) is a judgment from the SaaS deep-dive segments, UNSOURCED.
