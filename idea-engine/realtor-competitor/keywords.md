## Context
1. Sell: per-listing kits (videos + Meta ads modeled on local kept-running listing ads), Team plan, done-for-you.
2. Reach: US listing agents (≈1+ listing/month) and small teams.
3. Site: not live.
4. Competitors: VideoTour.ai, RealEstateContent.ai; also AutoReel (search leader).
5. Goal: free-kit starts and local-report emails. **Paid Meta is the main engine** (uvp.md Step 0); SEO is support.
6. Timeline: quick wins in 90 days; one long play.

## Top Opportunities
1. **Competitor comparison cluster** — "videotour ai" 720, "realestatecontent.ai" 260, "videotour.ai review" 210. Commercial intent. The "videotour.ai review" SERP has **no independent review**: a YouTube demo, a Reddit thread, two rival "alternative" pages (Avenue 510, Reeloft) and a directory. We hold dated public pricing/terms facts and 210 ads of run-length data nobody else has published.
2. **Real estate Facebook ads** — "real estate facebook ads" 1,000/mo, CPC $20.06 (highest commercial value in the set), competition LOW. Our differentiator (what lasts in the Ad Library) is the unique angle.
3. **Real estate video (category head)** — "real estate video" 1,600, "real estate video editor/editing" 390 each, "real estate video marketing" 210. Big, but the SERP is product homepages (Momenzo, AutoReel, WeVideo, invideo, HeyGen blog). Long play.

## Quick Wins (3-month potential)
- "videotour.ai review" / "videotour ai" — thin SERP, no independent page.
- "ab 723" (90) + "california ab 723" (30) — small, but the buyer is exactly our "nothing invented" angle, and our tool page can answer it. SERP is MLS/association pages (sdmls.com, pfar.org, CRMLS KB) plus small vendors (Plotpane, QuickStaging) — winnable for a practical "how to label AI-edited listing video" page.
- "open house video" (110, competition LOW, KD 6).

## Long-Term Plays (6–12 months)
- "real estate video" / "real estate video maker" — needs a strong tool page plus links. Product homepages dominate.
- "real estate marketing ideas" (390) / "real estate social media posts" (390) — HIGH competition; only with our own monthly "what lasted" data.
