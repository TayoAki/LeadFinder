# Ad descriptions and hashtags for our AI app builder

Written 2026-11-13. Built from the Keepwatch "AI app builders" workspace (780 ads from 16 rivals, 247 live) and the user-feedback report (`../feedback/REPORT.md`).

## What the data says to write

1. **Lead with "describe it, and it gets built".** Base44, Bolt.new and Lovable all keep refreshing this promise line.
   - Base44's "turn ideas into real apps" has 31 versions and has run for 259 days.
   - Bolt.new has 58 versions of "If you can describe it, you can build it."
   - It's proven, but everyone says it. So use it only as the opening, then add what they don't say.
2. **Our edge is the top complaints, which no rival answers in its ads.**
   - Credits burned on the tool's own mistakes: 256 users, 101 of whom quit or switched.
   - Broken builds and lost work: 253 users.
   - Hitting a wall once the app gets real: 136 users.
   - Apps that feel like a website: 100 users.
   - App Store rejections: 59 users.
3. **Creator-story copy is what Replit runs and refreshes:** "I built X for Y in Z minutes". Use it for creator/UGC posts.
4. **Rivals use few hashtags.** They're mostly `#ad` plus a partner tag (`#ReplitPartner`, `#LovablePartner`) and one category tag (`#appbuilder`, `#vibecoding`, `#nocode`).

> Only use a line marked ⚠️ if the product really does it. Several answer complaints with a promise, for example about credits or approval. An untrue claim in an ad is a policy and legal risk.

## Description options

### A. The proven promise plus our edge (best for Dynamic/DCO, which most rivals use)
1. If you can describe the app, you can build it. We research the idea, show you the screens to approve, then build a real native iPhone and Android app.
2. Describe your app in plain words. See every screen before a line of code is written. Then it's built as a real native app, not a website in a wrapper.
3. Type the app idea you keep talking about. Approve the mockups. Get a native app with sign-in, payments and a backend already set up.

### B. Answering the top complaint: wasted credits and broken builds
4. ⚠️ Other builders charge you to fix their own mistakes. We don't. Describe your app and we build it, fixes included.
5. Tired of fix-it loops eating your credits? You approve the design first, so the build matches what you asked for the first time.
6. ⚠️ Your app, your code. Every version is saved, so one prompt never wipes out what already works.

### C. The App Store as the finish line (most rivals stop at "it works")
7. ⚠️ From idea to the App Store. We build the native app, set up payments, and handle the submission paperwork.
8. Most AI-built apps get rejected for "just a website". Ours are real native apps, built to pass review.
9. Your app on your phone today, and in the App Store when you're ready. Describe it, approve the screens, and we'll build it.

### D. Native feel, not "AI slop" (complaints 5 and 6)
10. Real native apps with smooth transitions and haptics. They feel like an app, not a website.
11. AI apps all look the same. Show us an app you love and yours gets its own look, approved by you before it's built.

### E. Creator story (the format Replit keeps refreshing; post from the creator's account)
12. My clients kept asking for an app, and I kept saying "soon". Last night I described it, approved the screens, and it was on my phone before my coffee went cold. #ad
13. I've had this app idea in my notes for two years. Wrote one paragraph, picked the design I liked, and now my friends are testing it on their phones. #ad

## Hashtags

**Use 3–5 per post.** Rivals keep their winning ads light on tags.

| Use | Hashtags | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Always on creator/partner posts | `#ad` plus your partner tag, e.g. `#[Brand]Partner` | Disclosure is required for paid creator posts; every rival does it. |
| Category (pick 1–2) | `#appbuilder` `#nocode` `#buildwithai` | The category tags rivals already use. |
| Our angle (pick 1–2) | `#iosapp` `#androidapp` `#reactnative` `#appstore` | Signals "real native app", which rivals don't own. |
| Audience | `#indiehacker` `#founder` `#sidehustle` `#smallbusiness` | Matches the ICP: non-technical founders, makers, small businesses. |
| Trend (use with care) | `#vibecoding` | It's popular, but the feedback ties "vibecoded" to sloppy apps. Use it only on creator posts. |

**Ready-made sets:**
- **Paid ad (DCO / static):** `#appbuilder #nocode #iosapp`
- **Creator video:** `#ad #[Brand]Partner #buildwithai #indiehacker`
- **Small-business angle:** `#smallbusiness #appbuilder #appstore`
