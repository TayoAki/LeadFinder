Every volume is an **assumption to test**. No sign-up or conversion data exists. The first number to test is **launch-service bookings** (target ≥2 in 30 days).

### Unit economics
- **Launch service:** $299 ÷ ~3.5 h ≈ $85/h of Tayo's time, with near-zero COGS. Break-even is immediate. The constraint is hours. At 4/month it costs ~14 h.
- **SaaS tiers:** gross margin is **UNSOURCED**. It needs (a) the AI cost per credit and (b) EAS build and hosting cost per app. Rork's top-up price ($0.22/credit) is a ceiling reference, not our cost.
- **Free kit:** cost per generation is UNSOURCED. Cap it (for example, 3 listing sets per email per month) until the cost is measured.
- **Effort reduction:** once the automated pre-flight ships, the manual check in the service shrinks. Target 2 h per launch, which would put the service at ~$150/h, or allow a lower price.

## The offer (built): "Launch with Tayo"

**1. Achievement statement**
In one 45-minute session and the week after it, your AI-built subscription app goes from "works on my phone" to **submitted for App Review from your own Apple account**, with a checked paywall, a full screenshot set and a keyword-chosen listing.

**2. Likelihood**
- *Outcome statement:* Today you're guessing what Apple wants and finding out one rejection at a time. After the session you'll know every common rejection item on your app has been checked against the guideline it comes from (2.1, 3.1.1/3.1.2, 4.2, 4.3, 4.8, 5.1.1), and your listing is ready to be found.
- *Testimonial required (spec, none exists yet):* a solo founder whose app was rejected at least once before, saying which rejection the session caught or fixed and how many days from session to submission. It must be about **this** service, with first name, app name and a link to the live app. Collect it with consent after their app is approved. Do not use it before then.
- *Guarantee (honourable):* "If Apple rejects the app for an item on our checklist, I'll fix it with you and resubmit at no charge." No guarantee of approval. Apple decides.

**3. Accelerants**
- The rejection checklist mapped to guideline numbers (also the free page)
- A RevenueCat paywall check: price, period, restore, terms and privacy links
- The screenshot and keyword kit run on your real screens
- A demo-login and review-notes template for App Review
- For Android, a plan for the 12-tester rule (personal accounts) or an organization account. No fake testers.

**4. Fascinations (two)**
- "The paywall details that 3.1.2 rejections cite most, and where they go on the screen."
- "Why adding a remote paywall can trigger a spam (4.3) rejection, and what to change."

Fact base: both come from the research (paywall terms under 3.1.2; the r/iOSProgramming 4.3(a) case after adding a RevenueCat remote paywall).

**Price: $299 per app.** Reasoning: it sits far below agency rates and above a single Upwork micro-task. It is a test price: raise it to $399 if 4 slots fill twice running; drop to $199 if 0 bookings in 30 days with ≥300 kit users.

## Landing page skeleton (Launch with Tayo)

- **Hero:** "Get your AI-built app submitted, checked and ready to be found, in one session." CTA: "Book a launch slot ($299)."
- **Benefits:** checked against named guidelines · paywall that has the parts Apple checks · screenshots and keywords from your real screens · submitted from your own account.
- **Proof:** Weight Class (link to the live App Store listing). No numbers. Add real testimonials only when they exist and are approved.
- **FAQ:** Will it be approved? (No one can promise; here's the resubmit guarantee.) Do I need to rebuild in App Builder? (No. Any Expo/React Native app.) Do you submit for me? (You submit from your own account, guideline 4.2.6; we do it together.) Android? (Yes, see the 12-tester note.)
- **Bold CTA:** "4 slots a month. Book one."

## Right-person outreach template (only after Tayo approves contact)

> Hi [name], I saw your post in [subreddit] about [**their rejection / issue**]. I ship a subscription app myself (Weight Class) and I'm building a pre-flight for App Store review.
