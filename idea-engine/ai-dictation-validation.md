# AI Dictation — Validation

## Last Updated
2026-11-03. US Google volumes (DataForSEO; "×" = last 12 months vs prior 12), Google results (Serper), App Store (LiteScrape), Reddit (ScrapeCreators), Meta and Google ad libraries (Adyntel, ScrapeCreators).

## Verdict
**Hot: yes. "Selling itself": no.** Demand is exploding, but it's going to brands, not the generic category. The leader is buying its growth hard. Enter only with a clear wedge.

## Where the openings are
| Wedge | Signal | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Private / on-device** | Recurring Reddit complaint; open-source tools gaining | Strongest. Leaders are cloud-based, and Wispr's screen capture spooks people |
| **Windows** | "voice typing windows" 720 (2.5×), "dictation for windows" 880 (1.4×) | Most leaders are Mac-first |
| **Developers / voice coding** | "voice coding" 480 (1.4×, $13.80/click); Wispr's "Talk to your IDE" ads; churn in r/ClaudeCode | Wispr is already targeting it |
| **Accessibility** (RSI, dyslexia) | "speech to text for dyslexia" 210 (5×); r/RSI setup posts | Small but loyal; not explicitly served |
| **Medical scribe** | "ai medical scribe" 5,400 (2.4×, **$40/click**) | Real money, but crowded and regulated B2B |
| **Generic "better dictation app"** | Category flat; 6+ funded brands | **Avoid** |

## Recommendation
Don't build "another Wispr Flow." If you enter, pick **one wedge**: I'd pick private on-device dictation for Windows and Mac. Then:
1. Bid on "wispr flow alternative." It's expensive ($24.59) but it's buyer intent, and it's growing 6.9×.
2. Price against $144 a year. A lifetime license is the norm in this crowd: Superwhisper sells one.
3. Pre-sell for 2 weeks before building. The open-source alternatives prove a free floor exists.
