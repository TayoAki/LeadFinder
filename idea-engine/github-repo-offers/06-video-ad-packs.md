# Niche Scorecard: OpenMontage "video ad packs" for Shopify/DTC brands (Gate 0)

Date: 2026-11-10 · Offer: 4 AI product video ads/month from the brand's photos, modeled on competitors' winning ads. $500 setup + $1,000/mo. Claimed tool cost ~$100/mo. Lead: brands running only static ads in the Meta Ad Library.
Sources: Upwork job pages (via web search index, Oct 2026), LinkedIn jobs (treg `anyapi.linkedin.search.jobs`, US, past month, 2 queries × 10 rows), live pricing pages (Billo, Creatify, Insense), one vendor comparison page (Knowlify, checked Oct 2026).
Limits: Upwork and Fiverr search pages return 403 to direct fetches, so the Upwork numbers are a sample from the search index, not a full count. Reddit search via treg returned off-topic posts and was dropped.

| Check | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Buyers asking (20+) | Partial. ~8 tight Upwork posts sampled, budgets mostly $5–$250 | See 1. Strong in-house hiring signal on LinkedIn |
| 2. Competitors priced | Fail: saturated, and the price is high per video | 10+ priced done-for-you vendors, from $299/mo; self-serve AI from $10/mo |

Loose (in-house roles, LinkedIn, past month, US): PetLabCo "Video Editor and Producer (AI & Performance Ads)" (Oct 14), CGK Linens "TikTok Shop Content Creator & Video Editor" (Oct 21), OluKai "Junior Editor, Content & Performance", Tarte, Fashion Nova, Elm Biosciences, Brand Knew "Social-First Video Editor" (Nov 10). DTC brands clearly pay for performance video. Most of the money goes to in-house staff and creator platforms.
Trigger check: the lead source is brands with static ads only. **Not one** sampled post came from a brand without video. Every buyer already runs video and wants more or cheaper video. The trigger selects for brands that have not chosen to pay for video.

## 2. Competitors (live prices)
- [Billo](https://billo.app/pricing/): real-creator UGC from **$99/video**. Packs are $1,000 for 10 videos and $2,500 for 28. Managed service from $2,000/mo.
- [Insense](https://insense.pro/pricing): platform fee $400–$650/mo plus creator fees.
- [Creatify](https://creatify.ai/pricing): self-serve AI ads at **$10–$136/mo**. Pro ($38) covers 36 Seedance 5s clips or 56 avatar videos. Its "Studio" tier is a managed service for 25–100+ videos/mo, with the price not published.
- [Knowlify comparison](https://knowlify.com/articles/done-for-you-video-ad-subscription-pricing) (vendor page, checked Oct 2026): CreatStrat **$299/$499/$899/mo** for 5/10/20 ads/week. EditClub $1,600/mo for ~60 AI videos. Vidful $3,495 for 60. Brighter Click $4,500 per 12 creator videos. The CreatStrat price is not verified on its own site.
- Arcads: no public price on [arcads.ai](https://www.arcads.ai/) (the /pricing page returns 404).
- Fiverr has many "AI UGC ads for Shopify" gigs, for example [this one](https://fiverr.com/ecomwithrameez/create-ai-ugc-ads-ai-ugc-video-ugc-ads-for-shopify). The gig count is not verified because the search page is blocked.

Read: **$250 per video** sits above Billo's human-creator floor ($99) and far above AI subscriptions ($15–$30 per video at EditClub or CreatStrat volumes). This market is saturated (15+ offers), and cheap SaaS caps the price.

## 3. Margin (at his price, $1,000/mo, 4 videos)
- Tools: ~$100/mo, his claim. Not verified, and it depends on the model and how many rerolls you need.
- Honest hours per month:
  - Competitor ad research and briefs: 1.5h
  - Scripts and hooks: 2h
  - Generation from product photos, including rerolls for label and packaging fidelity: 4–6h
  - Edit, captions and music: 2–3h
  - QA, revisions and client call: 2h
  - Total: about **12–15h**.
- At $50/h: (1,000 − 100 − 600…750) / 1,000 = **15–30%**. **Fail.**
- At $25/h (offshore editor): (1,000 − 100 − 300…375) / 1,000 = **52–60%**. That passes, but the operator still has to do the selling and QA.
- The $500 setup fee covers onboarding, so it is excluded.

## Attention (30-day)
- The repo is real: [calesthio/OpenMontage](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage) has **79.2k stars** and is **AGPL-3.0**. It was #1 on GitHub trending in June 2026 ([nerdzap](https://nerdzap.com/news/openmontage-agentic-video-generator-github/)).
- It has **no dedicated ad pipeline**. The closest fits are its "Cinematic" (trailer, promo) and "Animation" (product demo) pipelines.
- Seller side is very crowded. AI-UGC gigs are on [Fiverr](https://fiverr.com/zeeshan_haiderp/create-cinematic-ai-videos), [Upwork services](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/video-audio-an-authentic-ai-ugc-video-ad-for-tiktok-instagram-amazon-listings-1998844624777735620), [Contra](https://contra.com/s/3mcYZk3A-ugc-and-ai-video-ads-for-shopify-dropshipping-and-dtc-brands) and ComeUp. Many tutorials teach OpenMontage. Course-seller counts are not verified.

## The video's claims vs the evidence
- "~$100/mo credits": plausible for 4 short clips, but not verified.
- "Modeled on winning ads": fine, though Creatify and CreatStrat already sell that workflow cheaper.
- "Static-only brands are leads": not supported. Buyers in the sample already run video.
- "$1,000 for 4": priced 2.5–10× above per-video market rates, with no proof that AI output keeps the product accurate.

## Verdict: ❌ REJECT (fails check 2, saturation and price cap, and check 3, margin at an honest rate)
Better angle (worth a RESEARCH SLOT re-test): target brands **already spending on video**, using Motion or Ad Library longevity signals. Sell "20–30 AI iterations a month of your proven ad" at $1,500–$2,500/mo, with an offshore editor. Narrow to one vertical, such as supplements or apparel, where product fidelity can be templated.
