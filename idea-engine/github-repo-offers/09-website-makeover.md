Date: 2026-11-10 · Offer: rebuild a dated or vibe-coded site for local service businesses and founders. $1,500 one-time, used as a foot in the door.

Sources:

- Live pricing pages: Designjoy, Durable.

Limits: Upwork job search returns 403, so the Upwork figures are a sample, not a count. Fiverr counts are not verified.

## 3. Margin (at $1,500 one-time)
- Tools: Claude Code or API, hosting for the first year, and a forms or booking plugin, about **$40–$80** (estimate).
- Honest hours:
  - Discovery and audit call: 1h
  - Content and asset gathering, and chasing the client: 2–3h
  - Build and design pass (Impeccable polishes the UI but does not write copy or migrate content): 5–8h
  - Forms, booking, local SEO, redirects, analytics: 2h
  - One revision round: 1.5h
  - DNS cutover and launch QA: 1–1.5h
  - Total: about **12.5–17h**.
- At $50/h: (1,500 − 60 − 625…850) / 1,500 = **39–54%**. **Borderline.**
- The "foot in the door" plan relies on upsells, and no upsell attach rate has been verified. A real 48-hour turnaround needs the client to supply content up front. Competitors put the same caveat in their FAQ.

## 4. Watering holes
- Local Chambers of Commerce and BNI chapters.
- Trade associations, for example state dental associations and PHCC for plumbers.
- Lead data: Google Maps listings joined with PageSpeed or BuiltWith (old WordPress or Wix themes, no mobile view).
- r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur.
- For vibe-coded founders: r/vibecoding, r/SaaS, Indie Hackers, and Product Hunt launch lists.

## Attention (30-day)
- The repo is real: [pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) has **107.1k stars** and is **Apache-2.0**. It describes itself as "the design vocabulary for your coding agent".
- It is a design-critique and polish skill: commands like `critique`, `audit` and `polish`, plus 62-rule "slop" detection. It does not build sites, migrate content or host them.
- Seller side is very crowded. Many AI-made "48-hour website" agencies are live (SiteLaunch48, Flashivo, Good Guys Group, Web Lift Up, Klarhimmel). A 30-day count of course sellers was not verified.

## The video's claims vs the evidence
- "$1,500 in 48h": a Sarasota agency already sells exactly this, and others sell it for $499–$699.
- "Impeccable makes it premium": it improves visual polish. It does not create copy, assets, SEO or migration, which is where the hours go.
- "Vibe-coded sites need rescue": plausible, but no buyer requests were found.

## Verdict: ❌ REJECT (fails check 1, few local buyers at $1,000 or more; and check 2, saturated with a $499–$699 floor)
Better angle: **one vertical** (for example med spas or home services) with a booking and review integration, sold as **$1,500 build plus a $150–$300/mo care and local-SEO plan**. Recurring revenue is what makes the foot-in-the-door work. Or test the **vibe-coded founder rescue** separately: an audit plus fix for a Lovable or Bolt site, which needs its own demand evidence.
