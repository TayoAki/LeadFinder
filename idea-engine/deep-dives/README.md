## What the research says about real estate

- **Agents pay for video, content and ads, not "AI".** Short-form video is the one cluster with 20+ recent paid asks (about 40). At least two buyers wrote "no AI edits", so sell the result.
- **Property managers and hosts pay for cheap human labour** ($4.50–18/hr VAs). The AI features they would buy are already bundled into AppFolio, Buildium, Hospitable, Guesty and Follow Up Boss.
- **HubSpot is used in commercial real estate, property management, homebuilders and proptech**, not by residential agents (they use Follow Up Boss, BoldTrail, Lofty).
- **Avoid:**
  - AI leasing agents, AI voice receptionists and AI voice lead callers.
  - AI listing descriptions and AI virtual-staging resale.
  - STR guest-messaging bots.
  - Wholesaler AI cold calling (phone-marketing law risk).
  - "AI for realtors" courses.
- **Compliance:** the Fair Housing Act applies to anything that talks to applicants or tenants. Meta's housing ad rules limit targeting. California now requires disclosure of virtually staged photos.
