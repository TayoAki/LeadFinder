## Takeaways
1. **Most proven outlier format:** a time-boxed beginner crash course on one tool, or an end-to-end project with the stack in the title. These outperform even on channels with under 5K subs.
2. **Fabric is under-served:**
   - YouTube is led by a few Microsoft-community channels.
   - On short-form it is almost absent.
   - On LinkedIn, Fabric and semantic-model posts get strong engagement.
3. **Partnership / sponsorship shortlist (reach + fit):**
   - **Fabric:** Guy in a Cube, FabricFrontiers, Pathfinder Analytics, Reza Rad.
   - **DE broad:** Data with Baraa, Ansh Lamba, Seattle Data Guy.
   - **Rising small channels (cheap, high lift):** Tom Bailey, jayzern, Thomas Hass.

## Caveats
- **TikTok/IG follower counts were not returned,** so those rows are ranked by top video views, not views ÷ followers.
- **LinkedIn is a 50-post sample per query.** Individual follower counts weren't available.
- **YouTube publish dates are approximate** ("x years ago").
- **Off-topic results were filtered out** (e.g. "snowflake"/"lakehouse" non-tech videos).
