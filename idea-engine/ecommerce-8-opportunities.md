## Totals (sum of the 14 keywords measured per area)
- **Bigger demand:** acquisition 504k (mostly TikTok Shop), shipping 280k, support 159k, retention 112k
- **Smaller demand:** conversion 52k, analytics 41k, inventory 24k, setup 15k

## What this says
- **Biggest search demand** is for known brands: TikTok Shop, Shippo, Klaviyo, Zendesk/Gorgias, ShipStation. Those categories are owned. Entering head-on means fighting a leader.
- **Growing, with no dominant brand yet:**
  1. **AI customer-service agents for stores.** Terms growing to 1.45×, the highest CPCs in the set ($150–460), and the results are a mix of small AI startups.
  2. **UGC creators for e-commerce ads.** 40,500, doubling. This fits the PromptNerdyToMe AI UGC angle.
  3. **Profit analytics.** Triple Whale and Northbeam are growing, and the Shopify App Store results are many small apps.
- **Avoid:**
  - Inventory sync: high CPC but shrinking, and taken by ERPs.
  - Conversion optimization as a service: "shopify cro" ≈ 0 searches.
  - Generic store setup: shrinking, and Shopify's own AI builder is eating it.
