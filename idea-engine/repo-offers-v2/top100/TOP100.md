# Top 100 open-source repos to build a 100M-dollar recurring-revenue offer on

Built 2026-11-11 from **150 candidates across 40+ industries**:
- **Repos:** every one checked live (stars, licence, last commit) through treg.
- **Competitor revenue:** looked up with a source for every row.
- **US buyer counts:** Census County Business Patterns 2022.
- **Search demand:** DataForSEO US monthly volume for "<competitor> alternative" + "<competitor> pricing" (526 terms; one-word brands like Harvey, Make, Square are excluded because their searches are mostly about something else).

## What "can reach 100M dollars a year" means here

An offer **passes the 100M test** only if both are true:
1. **Proof:** a named company already earns 100M+ dollars a year in revenue or ARR selling the *same outcome* to the *same buyer*. Whole-company figures (Salesforce, OpenAI, UiPath, Zoom, Adobe) don't count.
2. **Room:** US buyers × our monthly price × 12 is at least 100M dollars. `Share needed` is the share of US buyers we'd have to sign to reach 100M dollars a year.

**Score (max 10)** = proof (0–3) + room (0–3) + search demand (0–3) + judgement (−2 to +1):
- **−2:** the buyer already gets the outcome free or bundled, the project's maker sells the same hosting, or compliance blocks a small provider.
- **−1:** a real headwind.
- **+1:** a clear vertical wedge.

Each judgement and its reason is in `judgement.json`.

**Honest limits:**
- About a third of the competitor revenues are third-party estimates (marked `est`).
- Census counts are establishments. Broad "SMB" buyers use the 5–49-employee band (3.24M), which is still generous.
- The judgement column is mine.
- 100M dollars is a statement about market size, not a forecast.

## Read this first: the shortlist

The mechanical score favours **horizontal** offers: managed open-source replacements for HubSpot, Zapier, QuickBooks or 1Password, sold to every small business. They have huge markets and loud search demand, but they all fight a tool the buyer already owns, often with a free tier. They rank high on *size* and low on *wedge*.

The offers most likely to actually reach 100M dollars pair a **vertical buyer** with a **pure-play company that already hit 100M+ selling the same thing**:

- **#22 tastyigniter/TastyIgniter: Commission-free ordering site + Google ordering + SMS win-back** (Independent restaurants, 199 dollars/mo). Owner.com passed ~100M dollars ARR (Sacra estimate, Jul 2026; 2.3B dollar valuation) selling exactly this to exactly this buyer. 606k US restaurants; 6.9% of them at 199 dollars/mo = 100M dollars. Public phone numbers make outbound easy, and the latina-kitchen avatar fits.
- **#15 wazuh/wazuh: Managed SOC/SIEM for SMB** (SMBs (via MSPs), 999 dollars/mo). Huntress reported 250M dollars ARR (Jul 2026) selling SMB security through MSPs. Needs under 1% of mid-size businesses. Catch: a 24/7 analyst team, so services margins.
- **#23 Mintplex-Labs/anything-llm: Private AI on firm documents** (Law firms, 999 dollars/mo). Harvey is 190M+ dollars (company-reported; Sacra estimates 300M dollars by May 2026). 167k US law offices; 5% at 999 dollars/mo. Small and mid-size firms are priced out of Harvey and want their documents private.
- **#21 pipecat-ai/pipecat: Bilingual AI phone receptionist** (Home-service contractors, 249 dollars/mo). Podium's AI-agent line alone is 150M dollars ARR. 371k home-service contractors; 9% at 249 dollars/mo. Wedge: bilingual (Spanish) answering. Crowded.
- **#2 activepieces/activepieces: Managed automations** (SMBs, 199 dollars/mo). Zapier is about 310M dollars. Our wedge is done-for-you automation at a flat price; the risk is Zapier's and Make's cheap self-serve tiers.

**Pick for this campaign:** TastyIgniter (Keep the 30%). It's the only shortlist offer where a single company has already proven 100M dollars ARR on the same product, same buyer, same price band. It also matches the latina-kitchen avatar and a lead list with phone numbers. If you'd rather chase the biggest ceiling regardless of avatar, take private AI for law firms (#86 above), but the kitchen avatar won't fit it.

- **Licence:** 2 = detected by GitHub; 1 = read by hand; 0 = unverified.

A total of 5-6 is High, 3-4 is Medium and 0-2 is Low. See `confidence.py`.
