# Catalogue blog: simulated customer read

**SIMULATED, not demand evidence.** Each post was read by a rule-based stand-in for its category's ideal customer. It ranks posts and finds weak ones; it does not predict real sales. Real numbers come from the blog funnel events (blog view, product view, add to cart) once traffic arrives.

Built 2026-11-15 from 6047 posts. Store items checked: 1 product(s) in the store.

## How the reader decides

| Part | Points | What the reader checks |
|---|---|---|
| Their questions answered | 40 | Clothing: size, fabric, washing, shrinking, delivery, colours, cost, the catch. Everything else: size, material, care, delivery, cost, the catch |
| No supplier jargon | 15 | No "blank", "print provider", "your design", brand or model codes |
| Specific | 10 | Real numbers (measurements, weights, days) |
| Honest | 10 | Says who should skip it |
| Easy to read | 5 | Short sentences |
| Can buy it here | 20 | 20 if this exact item is in the store, 6 if the store has the category, 0 if not |

Buys: 75 or more and this exact item is in the store. Shops the store: 55 or more and the store has the category. Leaves: anything else.

The rules were written alongside the new posts, so a high writing score is partly self-graded. The persona panel at the end (real readers role-played on a sample) is the less biased check.
