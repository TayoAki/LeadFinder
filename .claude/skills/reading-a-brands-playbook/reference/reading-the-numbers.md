## The 30-day line
An ad live 30 days or more has been paid for through a full cycle of testing. That is why it separates winners from tests. It is a public signal, not an internal metric.

## Piles
| Pile | Meaning | Say it as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Running / kept | Live 30+ days or still going | "They keep running..." |
| Killed | Stopped inside 30 days | "They dropped within a few weeks..." |
| Retired | Ran 30+ days, then stopped | "Ran for months, then rotated out (often seasonal)" |
| Testing | Newest, not yet judged | "Testing now..." |

## Format lifespan
Per format: how many ads, how long they typically last. A format that is common but short-lived is a format they test and cut. A format that is rarer but long-lived is their bet.

## Small samples
Under about ten ads, say the read is thin and avoid ranking formats. Under three, describe, do not conclude.
