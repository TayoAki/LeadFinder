# Likely skip reasons

## Contents
- Plain-word reasons and fixes
- What not to say

## Likely reasons, how to check, and what to say
| Situation | How to check | Say | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Few tracked rivals | list the brand's competitors | "A drop needs a few rivals to learn from, and you're tracking too few." | Track a few more |
| Industry missing | read the brand | "Your brand's industry isn't set, so I can't match source ads." | Set it in the brand profile |
| Source material not ready | automation status | "Not enough current ads were ready this week; it resumes as more are collected." | Wait, or add rivals |
| Low variety | look at the rivals you have | "Your rivals look alike, so concepts would repeat." | Add rivals with different creative styles |
| Autopilot off | automation status | "Autopilot is off for this brand." | Turn it on in the app |

## What not to say
"The job failed", "the pipeline", "scores", "thresholds". A skipped week is a choice to avoid sending weak concepts.
