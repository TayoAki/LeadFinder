# Transplantable mechanisms

A mechanism is the *how* behind a winning ad or funnel, written so it can leave its market. Each entry
lists what has to be true in the **target** market for it to work (preconditions), what it looks like
in ads (how Keepwatch will show it), and how it fails. Add to this list from each study; never delete
a failure note.

| # | Mechanism | Ad / funnel signature (search_ads `q`) | Preconditions in the target market | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **BOGO / bundle** | "buy one", "buy 2", "bundle", "2 for", "free gift" | Low marginal cost per extra unit, *or* a second unit people genuinely use (gifting, refills, a partner) | Extra units are worthless to the buyer, or the market already discounts constantly (no anchor left) |
| 2 | **Quiz funnel** | "take the quiz", "find your", "which X are you" | Choice overload: many SKUs or options, and buyers unsure which fits them | Fewer than ~5 real options, or buyers already know exactly what they want |
| 3 | **Low-ticket front end (tripwire)** | "$1", "$7", "starter", "trial pack", "sample" | A bigger offer behind it, and a cheap first unit that proves the outcome | Nothing to upsell, or the cheap unit doesn't show the result |
| 4 | **Subscribe and replenish** | "subscribe", "auto-ship", "monthly", "never run out" | Consumable, or a recurring need (new content every week, refills) | One-off purchase; people resent being billed |
| 5 | **Free + small fee** | "free, just pay shipping", "free trial", "first month free" | Unit cost low enough to give away; card on file has later value | Freebie hunters with no follow-on purchase |
| 6 | **Drop / limited release** | "drop", "limited", "only N", "restock", countdown | Product can be genuinely limited; an audience that follows releases | Scarcity is fake (trust breaks the first time someone checks) |
| 7 | **Challenge / dare** | "challenge", "try not to", "level 1 to 5", reaction videos | A visible, filmable reaction or result | Nothing to see, or the dare is unsafe |
| 8 | **Creator / affiliate army** | creator faces, "use my code", whitelisted ads | Creators already exist in the niche and have their own audiences to pay into | Margins can't fund commissions; creators in the niche are rivals |
| 9 | **Lead magnet → email sequence** | "free guide", "download", "checklist", "free pack" | Considered purchase; a narrow problem a freebie can solve while exposing a wider one | Impulse product; freebie solves everything (cmba-journey kill check) |
| 10 | **Comparison / us vs them** | "vs", "instead of", "switch", "unlike" | A known incumbent buyers already compare against | No incumbent anyone recognises |
| 11 | **Social proof stack** | reviews, counts, "join N", UGC montage | Real proof exists or can be gathered fast | Proof would have to be invented (never) |
| 12 | **Membership / community** | "join", "members", "inside the club", "community" | Buyers want each other, not just the product; repeat contact | Purely transactional buyers |
| 13 | **Licence ladder / good-better-best** | "basic", "premium", "unlimited", "exclusive" | Same asset can be sold at different rights or usage levels | One-size product; tiers feel like a trick |
| 14 | **Guarantee / risk reversal** | "money back", "or it's free", "try it for 30 days" | The seller can honour it financially and operationally | Can't be honoured. **Never** recommend a guarantee the brand cannot back |
| 15 | **Referral, two-sided** | "give $10, get $10", "invite a friend" | Buyers know other buyers; the reward is worth sharing for | Private or embarrassing purchases |

## How to name a new mechanism

Write it as **[move] because [psychology], which works when [precondition]**. Example: *"Buy two, get
one free, because a third unit makes the price per unit feel like a win, which works when the extra
unit costs the seller almost nothing."* If you cannot fill all three slots, it is a tactic you
observed, not a mechanism you understand. Do not transplant it (Tuan Le: "understand why it works,
and whether your brand fits that answer").

## Worked example (illustrative only, verify everything in a real run)

Source: ecommerce supplements run BOGO and quiz funnels as kept ads (30+ days, refreshed).
Target: beat makers / independent music producers.
- BOGO → "buy 2 beats, get 1 free" already exists widely in beat stores. **Not a gap**; it fails the
  absent-in-target test. Record it and move on. This is the most common transplant mistake.
- Quiz funnel → "find your sound: which drum kit fits your genre" may be open if beat-kit sellers
  advertise flat product grids. Check with Keepwatch `search_ads` for "quiz" in the target set.
- Demographic angle: e.g. women producers, older hobbyist producers, gospel or regional-genre
  producers. Underserved only if demand signals exist (search, Reddit asks, paid intent) **and**
  the target advertisers' copy doesn't address them. Both must be shown with evidence.
