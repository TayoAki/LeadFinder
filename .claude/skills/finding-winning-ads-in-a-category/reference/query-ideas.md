## Category phrasing
Use what a customer would call the product: "meal kit delivery", "home workout app", "B2B payroll software". If the user gives a broad industry ("fitness"), run one search on the broad term and one on the most likely niche, not five.

## Related angles (up to 3)
Related products, offers or well-known advertisers in the space. Not ad formats and not internal taxonomy labels.
- "skincare": "vitamin C serum", "dermatologist-tested", "subscription skincare"
- "fitness studio": "first class on us offer", "boutique gym membership", "pilates studio"

## Seasonal asks
Put the season in the query as an ordinary phrase ("black friday", "cyber monday offer", "new year resolution"). Prefer ads that are currently running. State the dates you saw rather than assuming a campaign window.

## Reading the results
- Look for repetition across advertisers: the same offer structure, hook shape or proof type appearing in several top ads is the pattern.
- One standout ad is an idea, not a pattern.
- Mix formats: note when static and video disagree.
