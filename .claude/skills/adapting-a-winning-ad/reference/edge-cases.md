# Edge cases

## Contents
- Source ad problems
- Brand problems
- Cost and approval
- After delivery

## Source ad problems
- The ad is a video: the tools may adapt it as an image ad. Say so before quoting.
- The source belongs to a direct competitor and uses its logo or a regulated claim (health, income, before/after): adapt the structure, not the claim; tell the user the new ad will use their own brand facts.
- The ad cannot be found or opened: ask for a link from the library; do not substitute a different ad silently.

## Brand problems
- No brand saved: on a full connection, stop and point to the app. On a research-only connection the app handles brand setup. Do not guess a brand from the conversation.
- The user wants a different product than the brand's default: mention it in the plan sentence; the tool takes optional notes.

## Cost and approval
- The quote expires after about 15 minutes. If the user comes back later, quote again.
- A changed ad, brand or notes means a new quote and a new yes.
- Several ads at once is a separate, larger spend. Quote each and get one explicit yes for the total.

## After delivery
- Offer one follow-up: a different angle on the same source, or another source. Do not start it unprompted.
