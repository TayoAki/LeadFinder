## Why 30 days
A month of paid delivery is a full test cycle. Ads that pass it are the clearest public sign of what an advertiser trusts. This is the same line the brand Playbook uses.

## Building the piles from searches
- Kept: still running, or ran 30+ days.
- Dropped: stopped inside 30 days.
- Retired: stopped after 30+ days.
The tools return run length and whether an ad is still running; use those, do not estimate dates.

## Crowded and open angles
Crowded: the same promise or proof shape appears in several brands' kept ads. Open: a plausible angle (a different objection, outcome or mechanism) that none of the kept ads use. Name it as a hypothesis to test, not a fact about the market.

## When the sample is thin
Fewer than ten ads in a pile: describe, do not rank. Suggest a nearby category and rerun once.
