# Examples (illustrative numbers)

## "What do brands in meal kits keep running, and what do they drop?"
`find_winning_ads` "meal kit delivery" (angles "weekly subscription box", "healthy dinner delivery") -> `search_ads` same, no floor -> `get_ad` on five -> `show_ad_gallery`.

> From a sample of meal-kit ads: the keepers are simple. A photo of the finished dish with a first-box offer, and short videos that open on someone unboxing. The ones dropped within weeks lean on long lists of recipes and carousels. Crowded: "ready in 20 minutes". Open: almost no kept ad speaks to waste or the weekly planning chore. Try one ad built on the planning angle. This is a sample of public ads, and run time shows ads lasted, not that they paid off.
