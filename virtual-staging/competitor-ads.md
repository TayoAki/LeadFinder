## Read for a $25-per-image staging service
1. **Price anchors are set.**
   - AI tools advertise $0.30–$1 an image.
   - Human services sit at $30 (BoxBrownie) or "pay per photo" (Stuccco).
   - $25 is under BoxBrownie's advertised $30, but 25–80× the AI price. The ad has to sell **people, not software**: designer-made, revisions, MLS-safe.
2. **"Real humans" is the done-for-you message.** BoxBrownie owns it on Meta, Stuccco owns "done-for-you" on Google. Saying it alone won't stand out.
3. **Meta is nearly empty.** One active advertiser (BoxBrownie), and Collov stopped in September. Cheaper attention than Google, where 8 of the 11 bid.
4. **Nobody advertises to photographers as a reseller channel** except Aryeo, which sells software and doesn't mention staging. A "white-label staging for real estate photographers" offer is unclaimed in ads.
5. **Proof claims to watch:**
   - "73% faster" (Collov), "Harvard iLab" (Virtual Staging AI), "HGTV approved" (PadStyler).
   - Don't copy these; use your own numbers.
   - "MLS compliant / without breaking MLS rules" is a real agent worry and is only partly claimed.
6. **Styldod and Bella paused Google ads** (May and January).
