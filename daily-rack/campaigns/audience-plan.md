# Audience Plan — Daily Rack

## Last Updated
2026-10-28

## The gap
Learn-and-play American mahjong apps are crowded: 5 new ones in 2026, all rated 4.7–4.9. What nobody owns:

1. **Two- and three-player play.** About 9,600 searches a month; only forums and videos answer them. Daily Rack doesn't have it yet. **Build a 2-player (Siamese) mode.** It's the clearest product gap in the data.
2. **The step between lessons and a real table.** Beginners say they want practice "before I show up to a group"; experienced players complain about beginners at open play. Daily Rack's Pip lessons, bots and friends tables fit exactly here.
3. **Organizing the group.** "mahjong social club" is up 5.4×, "groups near me" 1,900, and "How do I find a mahjong group?" has no good answer. Game Night and friends tables with bot fill already solve "we have 3, not 4."

**Positioning:** *From your first lesson to a real table.* Practice with Pip until you're ready, then play with your people, online or around a table.

## Where to build the audience (in order)

### 1. Mahjong teachers (highest leverage)
- **The signal:** "mahjong lessons near me" 5,400 and "classes near me" 5,400, both 3× in a year; "teacher" and "instructor" 3×. Every teacher has a roster of beginners who need homework.
- **The offer:** a free "Teacher" Plus for instructors, with a class code that puts students on one friends table and a printable "practice between classes" handout.
- **The motion:**
  - Find teachers through the lesson results (Eventbrite, local studios like Mariposa, Lily Pad and Mahjong Society) and Facebook.
  - Email 50 and ask each for one class pilot.
- **Measure:** installs per class code; weekly active students.

### 2. Content that answers the gaps (search, compounding)
Ship in this order, from the keyword plan:
1. Siamese / 2-player guide plus a printable
2. Best American mahjong apps (honest, tested)
3. How to read a mahjong card
4. Cheat sheet printable (4,400 searches a month; the email capture)
5. How to find or start a group

Every article ends with the matching Pip lesson.

### 3. Short video on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube
- **The signal:** YouTube holds 3 of the top 4 results for "how to play american mahjong", and Instagram reels rank for card reading.
- **The formats:** "60-second rule" clips (Charleston, joker rules, reading one card line), and "Pip's puzzle of the day" (pick the hand from 13 tiles; answer in comments).
- **Note:** the app's name, Daily Rack, invites a daily puzzle habit. A daily "rack of the day" share card fits both the name and the channel.

### 4. Communities: Reddit and Facebook groups (answer, don't pitch)
- **Reddit:** r/Americanmahjongg runs "What apps do you love or hate?" threads, and r/Mahjong has "Why is there no good mahjong app?" (51 comments).
  - Reply as the maker, ask for feedback, and give Plus codes to beta testers.
- **Facebook groups:** they rank on most question searches.
  - Post the printables there, not app links.

### 5. Game-night virality (built into the product)
- **Today:** each Game Night invite and friends-table code is an acquisition link. Measure invites sent per organizer and how many invitees install.
- **To add:** a "Join on Daily Rack" landing page for invite links that are opened without the app.

### 6. Gear partners (later)
- **The signal:** Oh My Mahjong (60,500 brand searches a month) and The Mahjong Line (12,100) sell to the same buyers.
- **The offer:** a co-branded card-reading printable, or a "free month of Plus with your set" insert.

## Paid search (small test)
- **Click costs are low:** "american mahjong app" costs $2.97 a click with low competition, "american mahjong online free" $1.97, and "mahjong against computer" $2.63.
- **The test:** $300 on these, sending people to a "play free against the computer" page.
- **Hold off on:** competitor-name ads, until there's a review page to send them to.

## 90-day milestones
- **Month 1:** 2-player guide plus printable live; 10 teacher pilots; 30 short videos.
- **Month 2:** 2-player mode in beta; apps comparison and card guide live; cheat-sheet email capture.
- **Month 3:** Siamese online landing page; group guide; measure installs by channel, keep the top 2.

## Risks
- **Card licensing:** "Unlimited play on the 2026 card" depends on rights to the National Mah Jongg League card. Confirm before advertising it.
- **The market may cool:** American mahjong is a trend at 6× in 2 years. Build habits (daily rack, groups), not just trend content.
- **Lots of new apps:** stand out on 2-player play and groups, not on "learn mahjong".
