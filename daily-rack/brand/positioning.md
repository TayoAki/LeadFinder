# Positioning — Daily Rack

## Last Updated
2026-10-28 (from the live app at daily-rack-production.up.railway.app)

## What it is
An American mahjong app (Expo, web + mobile):
- **Learn with Pip:** an owl tutor teaching 10 short lessons in 4 units (tiles and reading the card → Charleston and courtesy pass → turns, calls, jokers → choosing a hand, scoring, defense), with practice games. Lessons work offline.
- **Play the computer:** 3 bots at Easy, Steady or Sharp; full or skipped Charleston; untimed; offline mode.
- **Play people:** quick match, ranked (Plus), leaderboard, quick-phrase chat.
- **Play with friends:** a table with held seats or a share code; empty chairs fill with bots.
- **Game Night:** plan a real-table game. Pick times, send a link, everyone votes.
- **Daily Rack Plus:** unlimited play on the 2026 card, hosting friends on it, ranked games.
- **Voice:** warm and welcoming ("Pull up a chair", "WELCOME TO THE TABLE").

## Working angle (from keyword research)
**"The app that gets you from your first lesson to a real table."** Learn, practice and then actually play with people, online and in person. Competitors stop at "learn and practice."

## Gaps the app doesn't cover yet (from search data)
- No 2-player (Siamese) mode. "siamese mahjong" gets 4,400 searches a month and "2 player mahjong" 3,600; "Where can I play siamese mahjong online?" is a People Also Ask question.
- No 3-player mode ("3 player mahjong", 1,600).
- No tools for teachers ("mahjong lessons near me" 5,400, 3×).

## Open question
The Plus tier says "the 2026 card." The National Mah Jongg League publishes the card each year. Confirm the licensing position before marketing "2026 card" in ads or SEO.
