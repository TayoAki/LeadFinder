## Segments (from search and Reddit)

1. **New learners: the biggest and fastest-growing segment**
   - Searches: "how to play mahjong" 74,000 (2× a year); "american mahjong" 22,200 (2.65×, up 6× in 2 years); "mahjong for beginners" 3,600 (2.4×).
   - In their words:
     - "I want to get a bit of a handle on it before I show up to a group" (r/Americanmahjongg)
     - "Even with hints on, I still feel like I can barely match anything on the card" (r/Mahjong)
   - Need: practice reading the card and choosing a hand, *before* their first real table.

2. **Class-takers and their teachers**
   - Searches: "mahjong lessons near me" 5,400 (3×); "mahjong classes near me" 5,400 (3.2×); "mahjong teacher" 720 (3×); "mahjong instructor" 480 (3×).
   - Reality: beginners take 1–2 lessons, then show up at open play. Experienced players vent: "why do beginners show up at games for experienced players, when they really should be taking a class or watching YouTube?"
   - Need: homework between lessons; a safe place to play slowly.

3. **Pairs: couples, two friends, a parent and child**
   - Searches: "siamese mahjong" 4,400 (2.3×); "2 player mahjong" 3,600 (2×); "3 player mahjong" 1,600; autocomplete "how to play american mahjong with 2 players for beginners". People Also Ask: "Where can I play siamese mahjong online?"
   - In their words: "my girlfriend is really into mahjong and I'm trying to learn it so we can play it together" (r/boardgames, 58 comments)

4. **Organizers and groups**
   - Searches: "mahjong club" 4,400; "mahjong groups near me" 1,600; "mahjong social club" 720 (5.4×); "mahjong party" 880; autocomplete "mahjong club app". People Also Ask: "How do I find a mahjong group?" and "Where can I find people to play mahjong with?"
   - In their words: "most groups are during the day… I'm in my mid [20s/30s]" (r/Mahjong, "How do I find groups?")

5. **Gear and gift buyers**
   - Searches: "mahjong set" 165,000; "american mahjong set" 40,500 (3.8×); "mahjong mat" 27,100; "oh my mahjong" 60,500.
   - Use: affiliate and partnership channel, not the core user.
