## Business Context
- Offer: Daily Rack, an American mahjong app (lessons with Pip, play the computer, online and friends tables, Game Night planner; Plus subscription)
- Audience: new American mahjong players, mostly learning to join a real group; teachers, pairs and organizers
- Goal: audience (installs and returning players), then Plus subscriptions
- Positioning: from your first lesson to a real table

## The market in one paragraph
American mahjong is booming. "american mahjong" went from 4,400 searches a month in Oct 2024 to 27,100–33,100 in mid-to-late 2026, about 6×. "How to play mahjong" (74,000) and "mahjong lessons near me" (3×) are rising with it. But the **learn-and-play app is crowded**: 8 American mahjong apps on the App Store, 5 launched in 2026, all rated 4.7–4.9. The gaps are in **who and how people play**: two players, a group to play with, and help between classes. Not another general "learn mahjong" app.

### Demoted
- **The card itself** ("mahjong card 2026" 18,100; "national mah jongg league" 9,900). The National Mah Jongg League owns it and sells it. Results are NMJL, Amazon and card sellers, and reproducing the card raises copyright risk. Link to NMJL, teach card *reading*, don't publish the card.
- **Gear** ("mahjong set" 165,000; "american mahjong set" 40,500). Owned by Oh My Mahjong, The Mahjong Line and Amazon. Affiliate and partnership only.
- **Mahjong solitaire** (165,000). A different game; the audience doesn't match.
- **Lessons near me** (5,400 each). Local businesses win. Turn teachers into a distribution partner instead (see the audience plan).

## Competitive Landscape
- **Apps:** Eight Bam (22.9k ratings) and Mahjong 4 Friends (25.8k) lead; The Mahjong App grew to 8k ratings in about 6 months in 2026. Four more launched in 2026. None owns 2-player or group organizing.
- **Content:** I Love Mahj, Mahjong 4 Friends and Southern Sparrow rank for learning; YouTube dominates how-to; Facebook groups and Reddit answer many questions.
- **Gaps:**
  - 2- and 3-player play online. Forums and video only.
  - "How to find a mahjong group." No good article.
  - "Best American mahjong app." Reddit #1; no tested, honest comparison.
  - Card reading. Facebook, Instagram and Reddit in the top 7.
