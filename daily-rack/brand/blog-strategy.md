## What the blog is
**"The Table" — the friendly place to get better at American mahjong, and to get a game going.**

One promise: every post gets a reader closer to a real game. Help them learn a rule, practice a skill, or gather their people, then hand off to the matching Pip lesson, a computer game, or Game Night.

It is *not* a mahjong news site, a gear shop, or a place to reprint the National Mah Jongg League card.

## Who it's for
New and newer American mahjong players (the "how to play mahjong" crowd: 74,000 searches a month, 2× a year), plus the pairs and organizers the app serves best.

## What the data says NOT to build as blog content
- **A daily puzzle blog.** "mahjong puzzle" (1,000) means jigsaw/solitaire; "mahjong quiz", "hand of the day", "which hand to play" ≈ 0. The daily rack belongs in the app and on short video, not in SEO.
- **"Mahjong blog" / newsletter as keywords.** ≈ 0 searches. A newsletter is still worth having, but for retention, not discovery.
- **Card-hand breakdowns.** No search ("mahjong hand analysis" 0) and copyright risk.

## Format rules
- **Answer first**, in Pip's warm voice (see voice-profile.md).
- **Every People Also Ask question** on the target search gets an H2 or FAQ answer.
- **Printables** where people search for "pdf" or "printable" (Siamese rules, cheat sheet). These are the email capture.
- **One clear CTA** per post, always to the matching place in the app.
- **Cite the rule source** (National Mah Jongg League, mahjongg.org for Siamese) and date every post. House rules vary; say so.
- **No reproduced NMJL card hands.** Use made-up example lines in the card's format.

## Cadence
- **One post a week** for 12 weeks (calendar in keyword-plan.md).
- **Refresh "The Card Year" posts every March–April.**
- **Turn each post into 3–5 short videos.** Video holds 3 of the top 4 Google results for "how to play american mahjong".
