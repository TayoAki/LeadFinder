# Voice Profile — Daily Rack

## Last Updated
2026-10-29, drawn from the live app's own copy (no /brand-voice run)

## Tone
Warm, welcoming, unhurried. A friendly regular who saves you a seat, not a rules referee.

## Personality
- **The table host:** "Pull up a chair." "Sit down to a game." "Deal me in."
- **Pip the owl** is the teacher: patient, a little playful, proud of small steps ("Mahjong means 'sparrow,' so I'm the right bird to teach you").

## Signature patterns (from the app)
- Short, inviting imperatives: "Play a game." "Set up a table." "Start lesson 1."
- Plain English for jargon: "Charleston, pung, joker swap and friends, in plain English."
- Reassurance about pace: "Take as long as you like on every move." "One small step at a time."
- Table language: table, seat, chair, deal, game night, your people.

## Words to use
table, seat, game night, your group, practice, rack, the card, mahj, calling, jokers, Charleston, "in plain English"

## Words to avoid
crush, dominate, hacks, master (as a promise), "pro tips", gatekeeping language, anything that shames beginners

## Rhythm
Short sentences, then one that explains. Friendly asides in parentheses. Occasional Pip line in italics for encouragement. No exclamation-point pileups.

## Example
> Two players? That's a game. Siamese mahjong gives each of you two racks, so the table never feels empty.
