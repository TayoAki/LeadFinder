# Positioning — Orchard Hour (proposed)

## Last Updated
2026-10-27 (from the founder brief)

- **Brand:** Orchard Hour, a proposed fiber drink brand
- **Idea:** make your afternoon break a small pleasure
- **Hero product:** Peach Lemonade fiber drink sticks; launch offer is a five-stick sampler
- **Positioning:** an enjoyable, simple ritual that fits between meetings
- **Personality:** warm, candid, lightly playful
- **Look:** cream, peach and deep green; soft serif wordmark; sun-and-horizon symbol
- **Tagline:** "A little good, every afternoon."
- **Unvalidated:** taste, demand, pricing; also the fiber type (psyllium, inulin, acacia, PHGG), which decides the gas/bloating and texture story
