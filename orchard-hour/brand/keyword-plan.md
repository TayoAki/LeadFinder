# Keyword Plan

## Last Updated

## Business Context
- Offer: Peach Lemonade fiber drink sticks; five-stick sampler first
- Audience: desk workers who want more fiber but quit powders over taste or texture
- Goal: sales (sampler, then repeat purchase)
- Positioning: an enjoyable afternoon ritual. **Use it as the voice, not the keywords**: the ritual phrases have almost no search

Plus the head term "best fiber supplement" 33,100.

- **Competitive: PASS on the long tail.** Reddit ranks #1 for "fiber gummies vs powder" and "psyllium husk taste", and #3–4 for "metamucil alternative". The head term is held by Forbes, Mayo and GoodRx (long play).
- **Advantage: YES.** Taste is the product, and blind taste-test data would be proprietary.

### Pillar 4: Fibermaxxing and daily fiber goals -- Priority: Medium (top of funnel)
Validation:
- **Search: PASS.** fibermaxxing 3,600 (3.5×), fiber calculator 2,900 (+42%), how much fiber per day 60,500, high fiber snacks 49,500.
- **Market: PASS.**
- **Competitive: FAIL on head terms, PASS on fibermaxxing.** Mayo Clinic and eatright own "how much fiber per day"; "fibermaxxing" is covered by health systems and NPR, with "fibermaxxing reddit" in autocomplete.
- **Advantage: PARTIAL.**

Verdict: one fibermaxxing guide plus a **free fiber calculator** (a tool, as the lead magnet). Don't chase the head terms.

### Demoted
- **Afternoon ritual / desk snacks.** healthy afternoon drink 0; healthy drinks for work 20; afternoon slump 880; healthy office snacks 1,600 (snack intent, not drinks). Fails search and is product-centric. Use it for brand, social and email, not SEO.
- **Prebiotic soda / soda alternatives.** 110,000 (1.9×) and olipop vs poppi 9,900, but owned by Olipop, Poppi, Coca-Cola and publishers, and it's a different format. Backlog: maybe a later "fiber you drink instead of a soda" angle.
- **GLP-1 fiber.** fiber supplement for glp-1 390 (+78%, $4.85 a click), and "What fiber is like Ozempic?" appears in People Also Ask on 3 searches. Real demand, but it carries health-claim risk. Hold until legal review.

## Competitive Landscape
- **Incumbents:** Metamucil and Benefiber own the brands and retail pages. Benefiber already sells **Strawberry Lemonade stick packs**, so the taste claim must be won in a head-to-head test.
- **Publishers:** Forbes, Mayo, GoodRx and Healthline own the head terms.
- **Gap:** Reddit and Facebook threads answer the taste, texture, gas and gummy-vs-powder questions. No brand owns "best tasting".
- **DTC fiber brands:** Bellway (−62%) and Supergut (−44%) aren't winning search. Room exists, and caution is warranted.
